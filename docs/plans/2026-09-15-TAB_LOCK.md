# Tab lock — 2026-09-15

**Status:** LOCKED by founder (second pass). Top selectors = these **11 analysis tabs**. Time windows live **on the card or chart they describe**, not in that bar.

Formula (not a tab): **Total ROAS = Shopify Total Sales ÷ entered spend.** Empty spend is not 0×. No pixels / MTA / OAuth zoo / Klaviyo.

**Settings** `/app/settings` stays Admin plumbing (targets, billing, Sample | Live). It is **not** one of the 11.

---

## Chrome law

The top bar is **pages**, like Black Clover’s SCOREBOARD / RETAIN / SPEND PLAN row — not Yesterday / Last 7 / MTD / QTD / YTD / Day / Week.

| Lives in the top bar | Does **not** live in the top bar |
| --- | --- |
| Overview · Customers · Growth · Orders · LTV · Spend Upload · Total ROAS · Channel Allocation · YoY · CPA · Goals | Period chips, grain (day/week/month), “this month vs last month”, year dropdowns |

Those clocks attach to **the specific card or chart**. Overview’s three YoY sales cards **are** MTD / QTD / YTD — that is content, not a global slicer for the rest of the app.

As-of + freshness + Sample | Live stay in chrome. Share stays on Overview.

## Per tab — job, paint, time, chart

### 1. Overview

**Job:** One glance: am I up or down vs last year.

**On the page**

- Three **YoY sales** cards only: **This month · This quarter · This year**
- Each card: this year’s Shopify Total Sales (same days) · last year’s same days $ · $ delta and %
- Missing last year: honest “not on file yet · ~60-day Shopify pull — not $0”. Never paint last year as $0
- Coverage line (60-day order window, returns included)

**Time:** baked into the three cards. No page-level period control.

**Chart:** none this pass.

**Not here:** typical-order glance, explorer, pacing, dual-close, mix, ledger, plan, Total ROAS hero, spend.

---

### 2. Customers

**Job:** Who already buys — dollars, not headcount.

**On the page**

- Hero: sales from **returning** customers ($)
- Rows: new vs returning $ · $ per buyer · guests · one-order buyers · concentration (top 10%) · orders per buyer · biggest orders

**Time:** shop-local as-of covering order facts on file. If a mix bar is added later, its range lives on that chart.

**Chart:** none required v1.

**Not here:** LTV 90/30/365 (that is LTV). Cash CAC / CPA (that is CPA). Growth first-time hero.

---

### 3. Growth

**Job:** New dollars and who came back.

**On the page**

- Hero: sales from **first-time** buyers ($)
- Rows: new customers · days to a second order · second order within 30 days · 2nd vs 3rd+ · second vs first order $ · repeat rate from **order history** · first-order-month headcount
- Link: Open LTV

**Time:** as-of / available facts. Cohort months are a list, not a top-bar year chip.

**Chart:** none required v1.

**Not here:** Klaviyo, email, subscribe counts, ads.

---

### 4. Orders

**Job:** What a typical order looks like, plus when/where it lands.

**On the page**

- Hero: **typical order** (median $; average as hint — Shopify Analytics uses the average)
- Sales clock: original · after returns · product only
- Rows: most-orders $ band · typical day $ · discounted share · full vs discounted typical · items per order · 2+ item orders · returns/edits $ · shipping + tax $
- **Then old Timing:** weekend sales % · busiest weekday (+ breakdown) · busiest hour (+ top hours) · biggest three days % · Online / POS / Shop mix + typical $ by source

**Time:** as-of. If a weekday/hour chart is added, grain lives **on that chart**.

**Chart:** none required v1 (rows first). Optional later: hour/weekday bars with their own range.

**Not here:** spend, ROAS, customers/LTV.

---

### 5. LTV

**Job:** What a new buyer is worth over 30 / 90 / 365 from **orders**.

**On the page** — existing `/app/ltv`

- Hero: first 90 days $
- Rows: first 30 · first year · first-order months
- Cash CAC **only if spend is already typed** — not required to use the tab

**Time:** first-order months are the clock. No MTD slicer.

**Chart:** none v1.

**Not here:** returning-sales hero (Customers). Repeat-rate deep dive can link from Growth.

---

### 6. Spend Upload

**Job:** Get spend on file. Input only.

**On the page** — current Marketing **doors + forms + ledger**, stripped of analysis

- Add a day · daily amount until I change it · import / CSV
- Coverage strip (filled vs empty days — click empty → add a day)
- Recent entries (edit/delete)
- Honest empty: Shopify sales already here; empty spend is **not** 0×

**Time:** the date on the row you type. Coverage strip is a day list, not a tab.

**Chart:** none. **No** SpendExplorer here.

**Not here:** Total ROAS hero, mix table, plan, dual-close, intel L7/L28.

### 7. Total ROAS

**Job:** Sales next to spend. The spend scoreboard + the explorer chart.

**On the page**

- Pair: Shopify Total Sales | Total spend | **Total ROAS** (formula visible once)
- Dual-close sentence (hidden at $0 spend or month-end)
- Monthly pacing (gauge + tiles)
- Intel: last 7 / last 28 Total ROAS vs prior window (range **on that table**)
- **Chart: SpendExplorer** — stacked channel spend + sales line + ROAS line + target + break-even

**Time:** explorer **range / grain / compare live on the chart**. Intel windows live on the intel table. Not in the top bar.

**Empty spend:** sales still show; ROAS is blank with “add spend on Spend Upload” — never 0×.

**Not here:** CSV form, mix pie, 12-month goals board.

### 8. Channel Allocation

**Job:** Where the dollars went, and the daily cap.

**On the page** — today’s Allocation + Marketing mix/plan

- Mix **table**: channel · spend · share · days · vs last month · vs last year
- Mix **pie** (spend share) — Allocation only, not duplicated on Total ROAS
- Plan sentence + daily cap table (last 7 /day vs plan /day)
- Best / rolling windows (existing Allocation)

**Time:** mix window buttons (**This month / Last 7 / This quarter**) sit **on the mix table**, not the top bar.

**Chart:** pie. Explorer stays on Total ROAS (do not remount a fourth explorer).

**Not here:** add-a-day form. “4.0 plan” as its own tab — that **is** this page’s plan table.

### 9. YoY

**Job:** This year vs last year for **sales and spend**, not the Overview snapshot.

**On the page**

- Compare table: this month vs last month vs last year (sales, spend, Total ROAS when spend exists)
- Monthly board: 12 months · actual · prior · YoY % (spend/ROAS columns only when spend exists)
- Channel vs last year (from mix vs-LY, not a second pie)

**Time:** year + month are **on this table/chart**. Missing last year: same 60-day honesty as Overview — not $0.

**Chart:** optional monthly bars this year vs last year, with the year control on the chart.

**Not here:** the three Overview sales cards (those stay Overview so the home glance stays three cards). Duplicate explorer.

---

### 10. CPA

**Job:** What a buyer cost in **cash**, from typed spend — not ads-manager CPA.

**On the page**

- Cash **CPA** = period spend ÷ identified buyers
- Cash **CAC** = period spend ÷ new customers
- New-sales ÷ spend (average, not causal)
- Payback vs first-90 LTV when both exist
- Link: Open LTV · Open Spend Upload

**Time:** window control **on these cards** (this month / last 28) — not the top bar.

**Chart:** none required v1.

**Empty spend:** honest “add spend” — never $0 CPA.

**Not here:** pixels, platform CPA, Klaviyo.

---

### 11. Goals

**Job:** Sales plan vs actual. Target Total ROAS lives in Settings; this page uses it.

**On the page** — existing `/app/goals`

- Sales vs plan
- **Chart:** MTD · QTD · YTD **progress bars** (those three windows **are** the gauges)
- Grow 10% YoY + extra %
- Monthly board (plan on/off, editable month goals)

**Time:** **year dropdown on this page.** Gauge windows are the bars. Not in the top bar.

**Not here:** SpendExplorer. Spend Upload forms.

## No 12th analysis tab

We already compute a deeper order book than Shopify Analytics Overview. **Every shipped number fits one of the 11.** Do not add Returns, Ledger, Timing, Advanced, Retention, or Discount as extra tabs.

### Pin as rows (already in code, name them on the tab)

| Number (deeper than Shopify Overview) | Tab | Why not its own tab |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repeat sales % (2+ orders **in this window**, not lifetime returning rate) | Customers | Same “who buys” page as returning $ |
| Guest vs account typical $ | Customers | Sub on guest checkouts |
| Top 10% of **customers** $ share | Customers | Concentration. Distinct from biggest **orders** |
| Biggest 10% of **orders** $ share | Orders | Ticket skew, not buyer skew |
| Typical discount $ · full-price vs discounted typical · 2+ item orders | Orders | Discount/basket sit next to typical order |
| Typical day $ · most-orders band (p25–p75) · shipping+tax % | Orders | Ticket + day, not a Timing tab |
| In-window repeat timing (days to 2nd, 2nd in 30d, 2nd vs 3rd+, 2nd vs 1st $) | **Growth only** | Do not also paint on Customers |
| Avg orders in first 90 days | LTV | Next to 90-day $ |
| Last 7 vs prior 7 (sales always; spend/ROAS when typed) | YoY | Operating compare; Shopify already shows yesterday |
| Hit rate vs target · remaining $ / headroom · dual-close · pacing | Total ROAS | Same scoreboard as the explorer |
| Product-only ÷ spend (net) as a drill, never the hero | Total ROAS | Religion stays Total Sales ÷ spend |
| Coverage holes · unmapped channel · Ads Manager recon · every-day ledger | Spend Upload | Honesty lives with the input |
| Largest channel spend share | Channel Allocation | Mix page |
| Margin-adjusted LTV / payback | LTV + CPA | Needs typed margin + spend; not a tab |

### Do not build a tab for

| Tempting extra | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Returns & honesty | Returns $ on Orders. Spend holes on Spend Upload. 60-day line on every page. |
| Ledger / every day | `<details>` on Spend Upload |
| Timing | Folded into Orders |
| Advanced Metrics (`/app/advanced`) | Tile dump. Fold into Total ROAS / CPA. Keep URL, hide from the bar |
| Sales-over-time at $0 spend | Shopify Analytics already has it. Our explorer stays on Total ROAS |
| “What to notice” sentence | Overview craft, not a page |
| Weekly recap | Share on Overview |
| Sessions, conversion, products, SKU, CRM lists, overdue-to-email | Extra scopes or email bait — refuse |
| Pixels / MTA / platform CPA / Klaviyo | Religion |
| Orders older than ~60 days at first install | Needs `read_all_orders` — not on the public app |
