## 1. Positioning + refuse list

**One-liner:** Mcfly Ads is the Shopify cash desk — sales ÷ spend, break-even MER, one Monday allocation call. No pixels. No path theater.

**Refuse in every outreach (never bait with):**
- Pixels, MTA, view-through, “true ROAS,” path / identity graphs
- Triple Whale / Northbeam / Rockerbox **clone** or feature-parity promises
- Paid link farms, guest-post mills, fake reviews, GMV-tax “cheaper suite” framing
- Public “type your .myshopify.com” install asks; App URL = mcflyads.com

**Ask for:** an honest look / mention if the thesis fits — never “please link us.”

## 7. Kill criteria (stop / delete)

- Spam sequences, purchased lists, or &gt;1 follow-up without a reply
- Paid link farms, PBNs, “guaranteed backlink” vendors, guest-post mills
- Fake / incentivized reviews; review-gating that hides negatives
- Outreach that promises pixels, MTA, TW parity, or forever-free bait
- Scraping personal emails against platform ToS; mass LinkedIn connect-spam

When in doubt: **don’t send.** Religion beats a link.
