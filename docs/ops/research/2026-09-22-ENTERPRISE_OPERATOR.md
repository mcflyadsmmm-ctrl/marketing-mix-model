## What a high-volume team would still pay to remove

Six pains clear the bar. Two more are real and marked HOLD so they are not cooked by accident.

### 1. The phone morning check is a spinning today, or sessions

**Their words:** “Just show the total.” And: “it only shows today’s sales and I can’t choose to look at the sales for yesterday or the week or the month.” — tonytapay, 23 Jul 2026. [Anyone else frustrated with the new Shopify app?](https://community.shopify.com/t/anyone-else-frustrated-with-the-new-shopify-app/654393)

Same merchant, 17 Mar 2025: “The app now defaults to sessions rather than total sales so I almost always have to swipe over to sales.” [How can I get total sales to be the default again on the app?](https://community.shopify.com/t/how-can-i-get-total-sales-to-be-the-default-again-on-the-app/401309)

Month pace, same surface: “I would find it really useful to be able to set it to ‘this month to date’ so I can easily see how the month is going.” — SteveG79, 7 Mar 2025. [Widget — month to date](https://community.shopify.com/t/widget-month-to-date-feature-request/399176)

**Tab:** Overview.  
**PASS.** First phone fold is Shopify Total Sales for yesterday, this week, and this month to date, as plain numbers. No spin. No session tile.  
**Why it is not a nit:** This is the open, every morning, including for a store that already watches the day live (pain 2). Shopify’s own workaround in that thread is “pin Analytics in the mobile browser.” A team that runs Saturday from a phone does not get the period it manages.  
**REFUSE** on the same posts: SteveG79 also asked the widget for gross profit. That is COGS. Do not add it. Do not “fix” the swipe by promising sessions.

### 2. Yesterday’s Shopify total moves after close

**Their words:** “Yesterday at 11:58pm, watching sales live and in real time, our topline sales were 49,600.00 (approx). This morning at 11am, yesterdays sales had decreased to 47k. Now, at 5:40pm, yesterdays sales are at 45,716.00. … I’m running the same report each time and triple checked the query.” — Wing-roro, 30 Nov 2024. [Discrepancy in Shopify Sales Tracker](https://community.shopify.com/t/discrepancy-in-shopify-sales-tracker/374962)

A later monthly-close reply names the same fight: “The dating mismatch is the hour we used to burn.” — lumine, 22 Aug 2026, on [the accountant-pack thread](https://community.shopify.com/t/how-do-you-pack-shopify-numbers-for-your-accountant-each-month/669948).

**Tab:** Overview.  
**PASS.** One named total, Shopify Total Sales, with returns shown on the day they post, so a drop after midnight is a return clock and not a silent rewrite. The morning line can be copied as that definition.  
**Why it is not a nit:** About $49,600 in one day is a multi-million book if it repeats. Almost $4,000 left the number by the next afternoon while they were “running the same report.” A team cannot hand finance a figure that moves with no sentence attached.  
**HOLD:** Do not build a second revenue that strips VAT or ties the day to the bank. Kove Footwear, Triple Whale App Store review, edited 6 Jul 2026: “Also Triple Whale just casually leaves includes VAT in your revenue (revenue should never be including VAT).” [Triple Whale reviews](https://apps.shopify.com/triplewhale-1/reviews?page=1). Mcfly keeps Shopify’s total and says so. It does not invent a VAT-out book.

### 3. Two Shopify screens disagree, and they still decide off the export

**Their words:** “Inside the dashboard, I see 5,958.06$ in total sales. When I look at the sales by product report, I see 6,648.60$ in total sales. The same number of 6,648.60$ is shown when I export all my orders from the 17th of September and add up the total sales. … When I look at the orders list, I had 281 individual orders, but in the sales report dashboard I see 251 orders. … This is really important, as we’re pulling Shopify data from the reports via Supermetrics and often take business decisions based on these numbers.” — Tudirad, 18 Sep 2022. [Why do I see discrepancies in my sales report numbers?](https://community.shopify.com/t/why-do-i-see-discrepancies-in-my-sales-report-numbers/152555)

Staff reply on that thread told them to export both sources and diff order IDs. That is still the workaround.

**Tab:** Overview.  
**PASS.** The desk states which Shopify total it is, on the card, so a 281-order day is not decided from a second unnamed screen.  
**Why it is not a nit:** 281 orders in a day, a ~$690 gap, and the numbers already flow into Supermetrics for decisions. That is an operator argument, not a rounding preference.  
**HOLD:** Do not reconcile Stripe and PayPal time zones inside Mcfly. Tudirad’s own follow-up landed there; it is payments, not the order desk.

### 4. “Are we on track” is a full prior day, so they spreadsheet the hours

**Their words:** “The new shopify analytics when comparing gives the full day which is useless, so for example it’s currently 2:55pm in the UK and I want to compare sales today up to 3pm on the same Saturday last year to see if we are on track but its only showing compared to full day amounts so now I need to download a spreadsheet and per hour and work it out myself.” — Kayleigh, 18 Jan 2025. [New analytics issues with comparisons](https://community.shopify.com/t/new-analytics-issues-with-comparisons/387984)

**Tab:** Overview, using the hour grain Orders already keeps. Not a new tab.  
**PASS.** A same-clock line: sales through this hour versus the same weekday last year through that hour, labeled as Shopify sales. The existing month close stays the written estimate (`so far + remaining days × typical day`). It is not this compare, and it must not be painted as Shopify’s forecast.  
**Why it is not a nit:** At 3pm a full-day compare makes a busy Saturday look behind, because last year’s evening has not happened yet. The workaround is a hand spreadsheet during trading. Weekday bars on Orders do not answer “up to now.”  
**REFUSE:** Inventory and reorder forecasts (Reddit threads about stock, lead time, and SKU velocity). That is stock, not this desk. Do not replace the visible typical-day formula with a black box.

### 5. Yesterday cannot be handed to the team once the store is busy

**Their words:** “I am trying to get The Total Sales from the previous day sent on email daily through “shopify flow” … just dont want to spend money or use another app on something as simple as this that should actually be built into shopify.” — BrendanB-YLS, 18 Jun 2025. [Total Sales from previous day to be sent on email](https://community.shopify.com/t/total-sales-from-previous-day-to-be-sent-on-email/420017)

He also wrote the definition he needs: “Total sales = net sales + additional fees + duties + shipping charges + taxes.”

A later reply on that thread, not the merchant: “The order query is capped (~100 records per run), so this won’t hold on a busy store. And “Total sales” as Shopify defines it — net sales + shipping + taxes + fees + duties — isn’t a single field Flow hands you.” — Acorp, 2 Aug 2026. Same URL.

Smaller cousin, not a Plus book: “We have 3 Staff accounts and all 3 are in use.” — CJackson770, 13 Jun 2023. [How can I give my finance team access to reports?](https://community.shopify.com/t/how-can-i-give-my-finance-team-access-to-reports/224216). And a new store asking to share “certain custom reports to an agency” without the whole Reports section — archit2001, 7 Jul 2022. [Agency report access](https://community.shopify.com/t/how-can-i-share-specific-reports-with-an-agency-on-my-new-online-store/133531).

**Tab:** Overview.  
**PASS.** One copyable yesterday line that matches Shopify Total Sales, so the person opening the desk can paste it to finance without a staff seat, a Flow sum, or a Zapier export. Share cards today cover returning $, typical order, days to second, and an LTV peek. They are not this morning total. v409’s “morning sentence” is still the open craft; this is the sentence a team actually asks for.  
**Why it is not a nit:** Flow’s own ceiling in that thread is about 100 orders a run. Pain 3 is already a 281-order day. The handoff breaks at the volume this scout is for.  
**REFUSE:** A Slack product, an email sender, or a sixth “Reports” tab. The job is the line, not a distribution suite.

### 6. A million-order history does not survive a pull

**Their words:** “I’m using the gem shopify_api v 9.5.1 and the REST API version 2021-10 to import a user order historical data, and It has over 1 million orders. As I use page_info to get to the next page, at some point I start to get HTTP status 500.” — MagnumFonseca, 27 Jun 2022. [Api pagination losing page_info](https://community.shopify.com/t/api-pagination-losing-page-info/131118)

Shopify staff, same thread: “it appears it looks like the query itself timed out which can happen with large data sets like this one, even if they’re paginated.” — Alan_19, 4 Jul 2022.

**Tab:** Overview for the morning totals. Customers only for order-level history that is actually on file.  
**PASS** the honesty: day totals and YoY from stored days, and a coverage line when the order book is short. Missing last year stays “not on file,” never $0.  
**HOLD** the crawl: do not promise a morning read of a million orders, and do not treat `read_all_orders` as shipped while Live is PARKED. A 24-month trial sentence is not this store’s year.  
**Why it is not a nit:** The book in the post is over 1 million orders, and the import dies mid-pagination. Any desk that pages orders to paint the morning will time out on the team v409 does not serve yet. Cohort LTV on a thin window is already honest; this pain is the scale of the pull, not another LTV chart.

### 7. Discount codes show uses, not the income — automatic titles are a different HOLD

**Their words:** “With out current business, we use discount codes to trade individuals sales performance. We track how much sales volume each discount code has generated. … All I can see if how many times each discount has been used, but I need to see how much income each has generated as well.” — jbradley2319, 9 Jul 2023. [How can I track the sales performance of each discount code in 2023?](https://community.shopify.com/t/how-can-i-track-the-sales-performance-of-each-discount-code-in-2023/231196)

**Tab:** Orders.  
**PASS.** Period sales dollars by discount **code** already stored on the order. That is this week’s income per code, for the people those codes track. It is not promo → later LTV, which stays on Customers and is already on the tip.  
**Why it is not a nit:** The codes are how this operator pays or judges people. A use count hides a large code that did little money and a small code that did the day’s sales.

**HOLD, do not cook with the PASS:** automatic discounts have a title, not a code, and the dollar-off is what a vendor demands. petgrocer, 3 May 2026: “Sometimes we need to report on a discount that was used 79 times. Right now I am manually going through all the sales and recording them in an excel sheet.” And on 4 May 2026: “I need to show the actual amount we gave the client off the retail price. That is what our vendors require from us.” [Discounts won't show up on reports](https://community.shopify.com/t/discounts-wont-show-up-on-reports/615788). A reply on that thread: “the ql query cannot distinguish between discount % and discount $ for that dimension.” — Maximus3. Discount-title crawl stays HOLD. Do not ship a ShopifyQL box, and do not print a percent as dollars.

### 8. POS-only today is still mixed into one total — source LTV is not the fix

**Their words:** “I’ve tried a couple of other reports like “Total Sales over time” (selecting “Today”), but the total does not reflect the POS. It seems to add together our POS sales, online store and other… fees?” — wilburnch, 14 Feb 2025. They used to match the POS “End tracking session” print to a channel report for bookkeepers. [POS-only daily sales report](https://community.shopify.com/t/is-there-a-report-on-shopifys-analytics-that-shows-only-what-was-sold-through-the-pos-daily/394689)

**Tab:** Orders, which already splits Online / POS / Shop dollars.  
**HOLD.** Do not recook source → LTV. Do not promise the POS session tape (that print includes the register close) equals Shopify Total Sales for `sourceName = pos`. A copyable POS sales line for the day is only a PASS if it is labeled Shopify Total Sales for that source and does not claim to tie the drawer.  
**Why it is not a nit:** Two books in one Shopify account (floor and site) make the home total the wrong number for the person closing the shop. That is a daily handoff, not a chart preference.
