**ShopifyQL they paste into Explore** (schema https://shopify.dev/docs/api/shopifyql/latest/schemas/sales_revenue/sales):

```shopifyql
FROM sales SHOW total_sales, orders TIMESERIES day SINCE -7d
FROM sales SHOW total_sales_returning
FROM sales SHOW total_sales GROUP BY hour_of_day
FROM sales SHOW total_sales GROUP BY day_of_week
FROM sales SHOW total_sales, orders GROUP BY new_or_returning_customer
FROM sales SHOW total_sales GROUP BY TOP 10 product_title
FROM sales SHOW gross_sales, net_sales, orders TIMESERIES month DURING this_year
```
