**Verdict:** Shopify Analytics in 2026 is a customizable Overview **plus** Reports **plus** ShopifyQL **plus** Sidekick **on every plan including Basic**. ## A. What native already does (2026)

### A1. Analytics Overview (default cards)

Help: [Using the Analytics overview dashboard](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/overview-dashboard/using-the-overview-dashboard) · [Analytics fields](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/analytics-fields) · Shopify’s own 2026 primer [Reports and Analytics: Beginner’s Guide (2026)](https://www.shopify.com/blog/analyzing-shopify-reports) (updated 10 Sep 2026).

Default pulse (and/or pin-able from the metrics library):

| Card | Native formula / behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Total sales** | Gross − discounts − reversals + taxes + duties + shipping + fees. |
| **Sessions** | Online-store visits (cookie-based). Session measurement rollout **21–23 Sep 2026**. |
| **Conversion rate** | Sessions that completed checkout ÷ sessions. |
| **Average order value** | **Mean:** `(gross sales − discounts) / orders`. Excludes post-creation edits/returns. |
| **Returning customer rate** | **Headcount:** `returning customers / customers`. Not dollars. |
| **Orders** | Count. |

Overview is **not** a fixed six-tile poster:

- Date picker: presets (today, MTD, QTD, YTD, last 7/30, BFCM), custom **date and time** (as narrow as **1 minute**; “Last 30 minutes” is a preset).
- **Compare:** previous period, **previous year**, or custom. Help’s own example: “Net sales over the last 30 days … or to the same period last year.”
- Auto-refresh every 60s when the range includes today.
- Insights (stores averaging ≥10 orders/week over 6 months) include **pacing vs the same period last year**.

**YoY on Overview is native.** Mcfly did not invent “this range vs last year.”

### A2. Customizable Overview cards

- [Customize dashboard (changelog, 14 Jun 2023)](https://changelog.shopify.com/posts/customize-your-analytics-dashboard-to-focus-on-key-business-metrics) — add / remove / reorder cards from a library.
- [Custom sections (changelog, 11 Aug 2025)](https://changelog.shopify.com/posts/organize-your-analytics-dashboard-with-custom-sections).
- Help: [Customizing the Analytics overview dashboard](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/overview-dashboard/customizing-overview-dashboard).

**Every report, including a custom exploration, gets a corresponding Overview card.** A merchant who can write (or ask Sidekick for) ShopifyQL can pin returning sales $ or weekday sales onto Overview. “Overview does not put X on one desk” is a **default-layout** claim, not a capability claim.

### A3. Live View vs Overview vs last year

[Live View](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/live-view) is a **different page**. It is today-from-midnight (store TZ): total sales, sessions, orders, visitors-right-now. **No last-year compare.** Do not conflate Live View with Overview.

Community (Jan 2025) still complains that **default** Overview compare reads as a **full day** vs a same-hour clock ([thread](https://community.shopify.com/t/new-analytics-issues-with-comparisons/387984)). Help in 2026 documents start/end **times**. Treat same-clock as Mcfly **packaging**, not “native cannot set times.” Do not claim Mcfly invented hourly YoY.

### A4. Reports (including Basic)

[Shopify analytics](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports): “main features are available to merchants on **any Shopify subscription plan**.”

[Basic plan](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/intro-to-shopify/pricing-plans/plans-features/basic-shopify-plan): **“The Basic plan comes with access to all reports, including the ability to create custom reports with data explorations.”** Staff accounts are what Basic lacks, not reports.

Shopify’s 2026 blog hedges “more advanced … on higher-tier plans.” Canonical product Help for Basic says **all reports**. Do **not** sell Mcfly as “Basic can’t run reports.”

Sales reports ([Help](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/default-reports/sales-report)):

- Total sales over time; **Group by** hour, day, week, month, quarter, year, **hour of day**, **day of week**, month of year.
- Average order value over time (**mean**, same formula).
- Total sales by discount codes; sales by customer name; by channel / product / referrer.

Customer reports ([Help](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/default-reports/customers-reports)):

- **New vs returning customers** = **counts**, groupable by hour / day of week.
- Returning customers / one-time customers lists (name, email, **total amount spent** — this is PII, not Mcfly’s opaque dollars hero).
- **Customer cohort analysis** (heatmap / retention curve): customers, retention, gross/net sales, AOV, **amount spent per customer** by weeks/months after first order, plus projections. This is native **LTV-shaped** reporting.
- Predicted spend tier; RFM.

Reports can be filtered, compared, visualized, saved as explorations, and **exported to spreadsheets** ([Using reports](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/using-reports)).

### A5. ShopifyQL (merchant admin, all plans)

[Create a new data exploration](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/custom-reports/create-custom-explorations): **available on all Shopify plans.** Sidekick will write the query from plain language.

[sales schema](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/shopifyql/latest/schemas/sales_revenue/sales) (fetched 2026-09-22):

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `average_order_value` | **Mean.** No `median_order_value`. |
| `returning_customer_rate` | Headcount rate. |
| `total_sales_returning` | **Returning dollars.** First-class metric. |
| `total_sales_first_time` | First-time dollars. |
| `new_or_returning_customer` | Dimension: New / Returning. |
| `DAY_OF_WEEK` / `HOUR_OF_DAY` | Weekday and hour grouping. |
| `is_discounted_sale`, `discount_code`, … | Discount reports. |
| `COMPARE TO previous_year` (and `previous_year_match_day_of_week`) | YoY in the query language. |

[customers schema](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/shopifyql/latest/schemas/customers/customers): `days_since_last_order` (recency), `total_amount_spent` (lifetime), `new_customer_records`. **Not** median days first→second.

Median exists in ShopifyQL for **fulfillment times**, not tickets.

Community median thread still unanswered as a native metric: [How can we calculate median order value in Shopify analytics?](https://community.shopify.com/t/how-can-we-calculate-median-order-value-in-shopify-analytics/254046).

### A6. Sidekick / “Copilot”

- Merchant admin AI is **Sidekick**. It writes ShopifyQL, builds explorations, and sits on insights. [Generate ShopifyQL with Sidekick](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/shopifyql-editor/generate-shopifyql-queries). Included with the Shopify plan ([shopify.com/sidekick](https://www.shopify.com/sidekick)).
- **Microsoft Copilot** in 2026 is a **shopper** checkout channel (Agentic Storefronts), not a merchant analytics product. Do not contrast Mcfly with “Shopify Copilot analytics.”
- Sidekick still hallucinates periods in the wild (2025 community). That is not a license to claim Mcfly invented YoY.

This agent did **not** execute ShopifyQL (locked).

### A7. CSV export of orders

[Exporting orders](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/orders/export-orders): Orders → Export. ≤50 downloads; >50 or by-date **emails** CSV (Total, Discount Amount, Created at, refunds, …). This is how merchants already compute a median in Sheets. Mcfly not requiring that export is a workflow claim, not “Shopify has no CSV.”

### A8. Sharing / staff

[Store permissions](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/users/roles/permissions/store-permissions): Analytics **Reports** and **Dashboards** are separate staff permissions. You cannot scope report-by-report. **Basic cannot add staff at all.** “Shopify itself is all-or-nothing Admin” is false on Grow+. Honest: Analytics staff see **every** report; paste/PNG skips a seat. Native workaround: owner screenshot or CSV.
