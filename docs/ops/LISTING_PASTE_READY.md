## Short title
Mcfly Analytics

## Subtitle
Deeper Shopify numbers Analytics doesn’t show

## Description
Mcfly is a sales-first desk for understanding how your Shopify order history behaves.

- See what a typical order looks like.
- See how much revenue comes back from returning buyers.
- Compare weekend and weekday patterns.
- See days to a second purchase.
- Explore order-based LTV, cohorts, product-to-LTV patterns, and whales.
- Walk the desk in this order: **Overview → Orders → Customers → Spend → Goals**. Growth and LTV are Customers chips.
- Use Spend only when useful: paste the spend you already have to calculate **Total ROAS = Shopify sales ÷ the spend you entered**.
- Explore the SAMPLE desk before connecting a shop; SAMPLE is not a live client.

Spend is optional. Mcfly does not claim Meta ROAS, pixels, or path credit.
