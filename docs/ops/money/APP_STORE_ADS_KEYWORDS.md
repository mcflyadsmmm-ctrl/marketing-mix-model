# App Store Ads — keywords

Land URL (only): https://apps.shopify.com/mcfly-analytics-public

Shopify does not let you customize App Store ad copy. Keywords must match the **listing**, not a different product.

---

## Bid (intent that matches the desk)

Aligned to listing search terms + cash religion (sales ÷ spend, not pixels):

- ad spend tracking
- total roas
- Total ROAS
- marketing analytics
- ltv cohorts
- spend vs sales
- ad spend
- marketing spend
- ROAS
- Shopify ad spend
- marketing efficiency ratio
- Break-even Total ROAS

Start narrow: **ad spend**, **total roas**, **marketing spend**, **ROAS**. Expand only after install-per-click is real.

---

## Do not bid (pixel-attribution confusion → 1-stars)

These merchants want connectors Mcfly does not ship:

- Triple Whale
- Northbeam
- auto-sync Meta
- true profit
- true ROAS
- pixel
- MTA
- attribution suite
- incrementality
- web pixel
- multi-touch
- Meridian / Robyn theater

Clicks with zero honest installs are the kill rule. See [`APP_STORE_ADS.md`](./APP_STORE_ADS.md).
