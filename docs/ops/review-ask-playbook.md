# Mcfly Analytics — review ask playbook (first 5–10 trusted installs)

**Status:** PREP. Run **only after** Live unpark + accuracy audit. Do **not** ask SAMPLE-demo browsers for App Store reviews.  
**Goal:** First 5–10 honest Shopify App Store reviews from people who actually used Live on a real shop.  
**Launched:** Sep 7, 2026 · **0 reviews** at prep time — reviews are trust infrastructure, not a vanity chase.

---

## 0) Policy hygiene (read before any ask)

Shopify App Store review guidelines — **honest asks only**:

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Ask after a real win moment on Live | Gate features / support behind leaving a review |
| Ask warm installs who already trust you | Offer discounts, credits, gifts, or contest entries for reviews |
| Make it easy to decline | Filter: only ask people who will leave 5 stars |
| One soft follow-up max | Spam every install on day 1 |
| Thank them whether they review or not | Write the review for them / ask them to paste scripted praise |
| Fix issues first if they hit a bug | Ask for a review while accuracy is still in doubt |

**In-app review prompts:** if added later, must not block workflows; never “rate to continue.” Prefer out-of-band warm asks for the first 5–10.

**If someone had a bad experience:** apologize, fix, invite them to update later — do **not** pressure a positive review.

---

## 1) Who to ask (warm only)

Priority order for first 5–10:

1. **Personal / founder warm circle** — merchants or operators Marty already texts; they know Live was parked and will forgive early edges *only if numbers are accurate*.  
2. **Prior callers / waitlist / SAMPLE craft partners** who said “ping me when Live is real.”  
3. **Friendly operators** who already run Shopify and will install on a **non-critical** or secondary shop first.  
4. **Agency / freelancers** Marty trusts — only if they install on a client *with client OK* and won’t treat SAMPLE as proof.

**Exclude for v1 review asks**
- Cold App Store traffic  
- Anyone still on SAMPLE-only curiosity  
- Anyone who hit a recon/accuracy miss until fixed  
- Competitive researchers / tire-kickers  

**Target count:** 5 minimum, 10 soft cap for the first wave (quality > volume).

---

## 2) When to ask (after first win moment)

Do **not** ask at install. Ask after one clear win on **Live** data:

| Win moment | Example signal |
|---|---|
| Overview clicked | They noticed YoY / pace on *their* sales chart |
| Orders / Customers | “Oh — returning dollars / typical order” reaction |
| Growth | Weekends or days-to-second surprised them |
| LTV peek | They saw order-based LTV and trusted the scope |
| Optional spend | They entered spend and Total ROAS matched their cash gut |

**Timing rules**
- Earliest: after **≥1 meaningful session** on Live with their shop data loaded and recon looking sane  
- Prefer: same day as the win moment, while the insight is fresh  
- Avoid: during onboarding friction, spend-CSV wrestling, or support-ticket heat  

**Dense tip / Black Clover gate:** if Overview chart + LTV still fail the Black Clover bar, do not run review asks yet — product owner holds dense tip; reviews should wait for craft that clears that bar.

## 3) Message drafts (honest asks)

### A) SMS / iMessage (warmest)

> Hey — Live on Mcfly is unparked and your shop’s numbers looked clean on my side. If Overview / returning $ / LTV actually helped, would you mind leaving an honest Shopify review when you have 2 min? No pressure either way:  
> https://apps.shopify.com/mcfly-analytics-public  
> (If anything felt off, tell me first — I’d rather fix than collect stars.)

### B) Email (slightly more formal)

**Subject:** Quick favor — honest Mcfly review (only if it helped)

> Hi [Name],  
>  
> Thanks for installing Mcfly Analytics on Live. I’m not running ads yet — just trying to get a handful of honest reviews from people who actually used it on a real shop.  
>  
> If the sales desk (Overview → Orders → Customers → Growth → LTV) earned a minute of your trust, a short App Store review would help other merchants. If it didn’t, I’d rather hear the critique.  
>  
> Listing: https://apps.shopify.com/mcfly-analytics-public  
>  
> Either way, thank you.  
> — Marty  
> mcflyads.com

### C) In-thread after a win (Slack / email reply)

> Glad that [returning dollars / days to second / Overview pace] landed. When you’re ready, an honest review on the Shopify listing helps more than any ad I’ll run later:  
> https://apps.shopify.com/mcfly-analytics-public  
> Totally fine to skip — product first.

### D) Soft follow-up (once, ≥5 days later, only if no reply)

> Circling back once — still no hard sell. If you tried Mcfly on Live and have 60 seconds for an honest review (good or mixed), it’s useful. If not, we’re good.  
> https://apps.shopify.com/mcfly-analytics-public

### E) What **not** to send

- “Leave a 5-star review and I’ll extend your trial / knock $10 off”  
- “We’re at 0 reviews — please save us” guilt  
- Pre-written 5-star text to paste  
- Asks to anyone who only saw SAMPLE
