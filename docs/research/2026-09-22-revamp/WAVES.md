# Revamp waves — hostile, then publish

**Why v1 failed:** `01`–`04` and `REVAMP_SPEC.md` treated the desk as mostly done and proposed honesty copy. Founder: the app and the site are not good enough to keep. A cleanup is not a revamp.

**Rule:** ≤4 agents at once. Each writes one file. Model for judgment: `grok-4.7-high`. Do not invent reviews or installs. No pixels/MTA. PCD L2 pending blocks Analytics-parity claims only. Reviews are the founder’s outreach, not a freeze.
