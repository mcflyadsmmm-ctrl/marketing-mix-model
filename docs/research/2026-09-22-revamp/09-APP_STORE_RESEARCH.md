## 3. Why Mcfly loses before the install click

Be harsh. These are pre-install killers, not post-trial product bugs.

1. **Wrong hero job.** Live subtitle and all five shots sell **ad dollars / Total ROAS**. The paid wedge and the intended paste pack (`APP_STORE_LISTING.md`) sell **Shopify Analytics depth at $0 spend**. Strangers shopping “analytics / LTV” compare Mcfly to Lifetimely/Peel/RCI and see a spend sermon. Strangers shopping “ROAS” compare to Keel ($9.99) or TW/TrueROAS and see no pixel, no OAuth, **0 reviews** — and bounce.

2. **Sample watermark on shot 1.** A 0-review app showing yellow “Sample data • example numbers, not this shop” on the first gallery frame is self-sabotage. Peers show polished marketing compositions or dense live-looking desks. Mcfly shows a disclaimer.

3. **Trust asymmetry.** Lifetimely **538** · Peel **38** · TW **91** · RCI **14** · Keel **1** · Mcfly **0**. At zero reviews, copy and shots must over-earn clarity. They currently under-earn.

4. **Anti-platform lecture before value.** Opening paragraph is a gap essay (Sales vs Ads Manager vs billboards) ending in “understand ad platform overlap.” That is founder voice, not merchant skim. Lifetimely opens on profit/LTV; RCI on retention; Peel on LTV/repurchase.

5. **Feature bullets bury the free job.** Live bullets 1–5 are Total ROAS, platform overlap, CSV spend, $39 allocation, break-even CAC. Nowhere: YoY vs last year, typical order, returning dollars, weekends, 30/90/365 LTV. Those are the reasons to install **before** typing a dollar of spend.

6. **Price story fights itself.** Flat **$39** is a real wedge vs Lifetimely order ladders / Peel $199+ / TW $219+. Live card spends that advantage explaining billboards and overlap instead of “deeper than Shopify Analytics, cheaper than Lifetimely, no GMV tax.”

7. **Shot craft is product chrome, not conversion craft.** Lifetimely/Peel/RCI wrap UI in headlines + bullets + (Peel) logos. Mcfly’s first frame is a raw Admin Total ROAS page with sample chrome. It looks unfinished next to Amp-class listings.

8. **Category confusion.** Categories tag ROAS + analytics dashboard, but the body fights attribution buyers (“No pixels. No path credit.”) while failing to win sales-desk buyers. Caught between two aisles; owns neither on the card.

**Bottom line:** Mcfly loses the skim to peers who name one job, show social proof or composed heroes, and put the morning number first. The live listing argues religion to people who have not yet decided the app is for them.

**Sequence rule:** Frames 1–4 must work at **zero spend**. Frame 5 is honesty, not the install bait. Never lead with Sample Total ROAS gauge.
