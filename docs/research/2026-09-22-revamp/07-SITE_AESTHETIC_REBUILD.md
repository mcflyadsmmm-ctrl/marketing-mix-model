# Site aesthetic rebuild — trust enough to install $39

**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Live probed:** https://mcflyads.com (meta `mcfly-version` **v42** / `craft-steal-v42`), `/pricing`, `/demo`, `/about`  
**Local ship tree:** `marketing-mix-model/site/` (branch source lags live; design against **live**)  
**Brand locks:** Public name **Mcfly Analytics** · firm **Mcfly Ads** in footer · line **spend next to real Shopify sales**  
**Honesty locks:** Reviews **0** (do not invent) · no install counts · no Custom Data Solutions sell · **no ShopifyQL / Analytics parity claim** while PCD L2 is pending  

**Chosen headline:** Spend next to real Shopify sales.

---

## Verdict

Compressing the hero is not the revamp. Live v42 reads like a compliance affidavit stapled to a product shot. A stranger deciding on a **$39** Shopify install with **0 reviews** needs calm proof of the job — not a first-viewport essay about what Shopify already does, what Polar costs, or that the App Store card is out of date.

Trust here is craft + clarity + a visible desk, not more monospace caveats.

## 1. Hostile read (live copy, quoted)

### First viewport — home

What a cold visitor actually sees above the fold:

- Kicker: **“Median ticket · spend optional”**
- H1: **“Deeper Shopify numbers Analytics does not show.”**
- Lede: **“Typical order is the median — Shopify AOV is the mean. Returning dollars sit next to ShopifyQL returning sales $. Spend optional last — empty paints —.”**
- CTAs: **Install** · **Try the demo** · price line **“$39/store/mo after 7-day trial”**
- Proof chips: **“Reviews 0”** · **“$39 after 7-day”** · **“No pixel”** · link **“App Store card still says ad spend”**
- Desk chrome: Snowdevil · SAMPLE chip · **“As-of Sep 16”** · three KPIs · YoY cards · caption **“SAMPLE · frozen Sep 1–16 still · not the live /demo clock · click a card · empty = —”**
- Facts strip still in first scroll: **“Reviews 0”** / **“Listing live · we do not invent a 4.9”**

**Hostile take:** The brand name is small in the nav; the H1 is a negation (“does not show”). The lede teaches vocabulary (median vs mean, ShopifyQL, em dash emptiness) before it sells a job. **Reviews 0** and **listing mismatch** are hero content — that is founder-nervous honesty, not merchant trust. ShopifyQL in the lede overruns Live capability while L2 is pending. The interactive SAMPLE desk is dense, captioned like a disclaimer, and competes with the headline instead of proving it.

### Pricing

- H1: **“7-day free trial. Then $39/month.”** (clear — keep the shape)
- Immediate wedge: **“$39 stays $39 at $5M. Path suites often tax GMV. Mcfly does not.”**
- Plan card includes **“Reviews 0 · listing live · we do not invent a 4.9”** as a row next to fee facts
- Mid-page tax band: **“A desk fee shouldn’t climb with GMV.”** + full competitor fee table naming Polar / Triple Whale / Lifetimely / TrueProfit list prices, ending with **“The live App Store card still leads with ad spend next to store sales.”**

**Hostile take:** Price itself is simple and good. The page then spends trust capital on competitor price essays and listing self-conflict. A stranger shopping $39 wants one plan, what’s in / not in, uninstall stops the charge — not a roundup.

### Demo

- H1: **“Full Snowdevil SAMPLE demo.”**
- Lede stacks: **“SAMPLE Snowdevil — not a live client… This is not your store.”** then iframe captions about same-clock vs Sep 1–16 stills, break-even margin math, Total ROAS formula

**Hostile take:** SAMPLE framing is correct in intent and wrong in tone. It sounds like the shop is broken or fake-suspicious, not like a deliberate showcase catalog. Two captions under the iframe re-litigate Home stills vs live clock — operator notes, not marketing.

### About

- H1: **“Mcfly Ads builds Mcfly Analytics.”** (firm/product naming is right)
- Mentions parked Custom Data Solutions in a “nothing adjacent” list — keep the sell off CDS; do not foreground it.

## 2. Steal structure from sites that convert (craft only)

Study these for **information architecture, product theater, and calm confidence** — not their claims, stars, or install numbers.

| Site | URL | Steal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Linear** | https://linear.app | Product *is* the page: one job sentence, then a large living product surface. Minimal nav. No apology strip. Sections each do one job. Type and spacing signal “finished company.” |
| **Attio** | https://attio.com | Product theater as proof — UI scenes that feel inevitable. Quiet brand, loud product. Short section headlines. Trust without dumping caveats in the hero. |
| **Littledata** | https://www.littledata.io | Shopify-native clarity: name the job in plain English (“data layer for Shopify”), then benefits in sequence. Installation path is obvious. Proof sits *after* the promise. |
| **TrueProfit** | https://www.trueprofit.io | Shopify-app marketing that leads with a **dashboard hero** and a single outcome (profit). Feature blocks under the fold. Price/complexity live elsewhere — home sells the desk, not the industry map. |

**Do not steal:** invented social proof, “trusted by N brands,” competitor roast tables above the fold, AI-agent hype (off religion).

## 3. Information architecture (short site)

Keep four public routes. Everything else is support / legal / parked SEO — not the install funnel.

| Route | Job | Stranger question answered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **/** | Want this desk? | What is it, what does it look like, what does it cost to try? |
| **/pricing** | Will I get surprised? | One plan, trial length, history windows, what’s excluded |
| **/demo** | Show me without installing | SAMPLE showcase → same chrome as Admin |
| **/about** | Who made this? | Mcfly Ads · Utah · desk only · contact |

Nav stays: **Demo · Pricing · About · Install** (Install → listing). Footer: **Mcfly Ads** firm line + legal.

### Delete from current home (or demote hard)

Delete from the **install path** (home first screen + primary scroll), not necessarily from FAQ:

1. Hero chips **Reviews 0** / **App Store card still says ad spend**
2. Facts-strip repeat of **Reviews 0 · we do not invent a 4.9**
3. Section **“Native reports, an LTV app, a pixel suite, or this desk”** + entire competitor **from-price** table
4. FAQ accordion titled like a legal brief on home (**“Does the App Store card match this desk?”**, Polar/Triple Whale fee paragraphs) — move to `/faq` only
5. Duplicate SAMPLE numeric theater (Slack copy block + Total ROAS + Goals + $0-spend KPI strip) — pick **one** order-book proof section and **one** spend-optional section
6. Any **ShopifyQL** / “sit next to ShopifyQL” / “Analytics parity” phrasing while L2 pending
7. Self-owning YoY ledes like **“Not a missing Overview metric”** in the hero desk
8. Custom Data Solutions mentions on marketing surfaces (About may keep a single “desk only” line; never sell CDS)

**Keep, but rewrite:** Order-book depth (median, returning $, weekend mix), optional Total ROAS formula, flat $39 + 7-day, no pixel, SAMPLE honesty, Install + Demo CTAs.

## 5. Visual system

### Direction

Keep **light editorial** (paper room, cool sky wash) — the *palette idea* is right for a Shopify desk. Replace the **execution**: live v42 uses paper/sky as a backdrop for **mono status theater** and a cramped interactive widget. Better execution = more white, fewer labels, product frame as the hero image plane, type hierarchy that a stranger can parse in two seconds.

If craft still feels thin after one publish, swap the sky wash for a quieter flat paper (`#F7F8FA`) with a single sky accent on period controls only — same brand, less “gradient apology.”

### Type

| Role | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Display | Keep **Bricolage Grotesque** for H1/H2 — distinctive, already branded |
| Body | **Figtree** (or current sans) at 17–18px, longer measure (~36–42ch for ledes) |
| Mono | **IBM Plex Mono** only for *numbers inside the product frame* and rare formulas — **not** for kickers, chips, or “REVIEWS 0” |
| H1 size | Large enough to own the left column; max ~12–14ch so the line doesn’t wrap into a manifesto |

### Color

| Token | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Ink `#0A1221` | Text, primary Install button |
| Paper `#FFFFFF` / page `#F2F5F8` | Surfaces |
| Sky cyan `#1AB8C4` | Accents, focus, SAMPLE period control — sparingly |
| Mute `#5F6B78` | Subcopy only |
| **No** purple SaaS gradients, no glow, no pill-chip farms |

### Spacing

- First viewport: brand + one H1 + one sub + CTA pair + one product frame. Stop.  
- Section rhythm: `clamp(4rem, 10vw, 7rem)` between major sections — current pages feel stacked essays.  
- Radius: keep sharp 2px product language; do not round into consumer-app pills.

### Imagery

- Prefer **frozen Admin-quality stills** (or a static crop of SAMPLE) on Home.  
- Full interactive desk belongs on **/demo**.  
- SAMPLE badge: small, product-chrome orange/ink — framed as “showcase store,” not hazard tape.  
- Do not invent merchant logos, review quotes, or install counters.

## 9. What not to do

- Monospace **status chips** in the hero (`REVIEWS 0`, `NO PIXEL`, etc.)
- Competitor **price essays** or roundup tables above the fold (or as the emotional center of Pricing)
- **Listing-mismatch** (“App Store card still says ad spend”) as hero content
- H1 that is a **negation** of Shopify Analytics
- **ShopifyQL parity** / “sit next to ShopifyQL” while PCD L2 is pending
- Invented **reviews**, stars, or install counts
- Selling **Custom Data Solutions**
- Replacing the site with another densify pass of caveats (“compress hero 10%”)
- Dark-mode purple SaaS look, glow, emoji, pill farms
- Making SAMPLE sound like a **broken live shop**

## 10. Success check (stranger test)

A merchant who has never heard of Mcfly should, in ~15 seconds:

1. Know the product name is **Mcfly Analytics**  
2. Understand the job: **spend next to real Shopify sales** (orders first, spend optional)  
3. See a calm desk  
4. See **Install** and **$39 after 7-day**  
5. Not leave thinking the listing is untrustworthy or the demo store is broken  

Reviews stay **0** until earned. Honesty lives below, once, in plain language.
