# Lifetimely / Amp Analytics — steal vs refuse map

**Fetched:** 2026-09-18  
**Start URL:** https://useamp.com/products/analytics/  
**Mcfly listing (ours):** https://apps.shopify.com/mcfly-analytics-public · 7-day trial, then **$39**/store/month  
**Religion lock:** Total ROAS = Shopify sales ÷ **entered** spend; empty spend = **—** not 0×; **no** Meta/Google/TikTok OAuth, pixels, or MTA zoo.  
**Do not invent:** Lifetimely/Amp install counts or review scores beyond marketing claims quoted below. Mcfly reviews stay **0** unless Partner says otherwise.

---

## 1. Verdict

Yes — Mcfly can be the better *honest* version for the merchant who wants spend next to sales without a connector zoo. Lifetimely sells a full profit OS (P&L + CAC/LTV + journeys + attributed ROAS + AI Profit Agent) priced on **order volume** from Free→$999. Mcfly already owns the wedge Lifetimely buries under integrations: typed/CSV spend → Total ROAS that never lies when spend is empty. Steal their **site craft** (dark chrome, product-shot heroes, Detect→Explain→Decide rhythm) and deepen Shopify five + Spend six; refuse Profit Agent theater, first/last-touch ROAS columns, ad OAuth, and GMV/order pricing.

## 4. Site / listing craft to copy (not product scope)

Steal presentation; do not clone their OS.

1. **Dark chrome + one accent** — Near-black canvas, white type, mint/neon green primary CTA (their green button + green metric highlight on LTV page). High contrast product UI cards floating in hero.
2. **Hero = product chrome, not abstract art** — Floating desk cards with real-looking metrics (e.g. “New customers 4,662 · +42%”). Mcfly: Overview YoY / Total ROAS chips / LTV from Snowdevil demo — honest empties as —.
3. **Copy rhythm** — Short personification headline (“hired a data analyst”) → one paragraph of watch/cause/evidence/approval → dual CTA (Trial primary, Demo secondary). Mcfly analogue: “Spend next to sales” / “Total ROAS you can trust” — not “Profit Agent.”
4. **Three-step story block** — Detect → Explain → Decide (or Connect → See → Decide). Maps cleanly to: Upload spend → see Total ROAS → hit Goals.
5. **Signal / Evidence / Next move cards** — Structured finding layout. Craft for listing stills and empty-state honesty, not an AI agent.
6. **Product-tree IA** — One hub + five deep feature URLs. Mcfly site can mirror **Shopify five** and **Spend six** as shallow anchors without building Amp’s depth.
7. **Logo strip under hero** — Grayscale brand row. Only real logos; Amp shows Bondi Boost, Bulletproof, Carnivore Snax, Chomps, etc. on fetch.
8. **Pricing craft to refuse** — Order slider + “six figures of hidden profit” is persuasive and antithetical to flat $39. Keep Mcfly pricing boring and flat.
9. **Marquee / partner banner** — Their orange “KLAVIYO PREFERRED PARTNER” ticker. Steal *pattern* only if Mcfly earns a real badge; do not fake partner status.
10. **Trial CTA microcopy** — “Just let me in – Start Free Trial ›” reduces friction. Useful listing/site line; trial length stays **7-day** (theirs is **14-day** on paid).

No pixels. No attributed ROAS as hero. No connector zoo.

1. **Spend Upload excellence** — Typed + CSV daily spend; channel labels merchant-owned; dual-close / ledger honesty.

3. **Overview / YoY first viewport** — Beat Shopify Analytics at $0 spend (religion: Shopify five must work before spend).
4. **Listing + mcflyads.com craft** — Dark chrome product shots from Live/Snowdevil demo; copy rhythm from §4; no invented reviews/installs.
5. **Goals + CPA** — Spend six completeness so media buyers feel Allocation/CPA/Goals without platform ROAS.

### P1 — Deepen Shopify five (OrderFact 24mo)

1. **LTV depth** — Cohort windows and product/first-purchase slices already implied by Amp’s LTV page; stay observational.
2. **Customers / Growth repurchase** — 30/90/180/365 repurchase from OrderFact (journey steal without acquisition ROAS).
3. **Orders clarity** — Typical order, tags, honesty when history still filling.

### P3 — Explicitly later / only with religion challenge

1. Shopify-native UTM *browse* (not attributed ROAS columns).
