## Message hierarchy

1. Deeper Shopify sales and order numbers first.
2. No spend is required to explore sales.
3. Type spend or import CSV only when Total ROAS is needed.
4. Total ROAS is Shopify Total Sales ÷ spend for the same period.
5. Harbor Home Co demonstrates the product with clearly labeled SAMPLE data.
6. Seven-day free trial, then a flat $39/month.

## Supporting proof points

- Shopify Total Sales after returns, orders, and AOV.
- New vs returning sales, goals, period pace, and cohort LTV.
- Optional contribution margin adds break-even.
- Merchant-entered spend includes typed entries and daily CSV imports.
- SAMPLE is product preview data, not a merchant result or testimonial.

## Ban list

- Reviews, ratings, customer quotes, or install counts.
- Claims that Harbor Home Co is a real customer or achieved result.
- Ad-platform OAuth or automatic spend sync.
- Pixel, MTA, path-credit, or ad-platform-attributed-sales claims.
- “True ROAS,” causal channel ROAS, or attribution language.
- Free/Pro tiers, paid-only suffixes, or any plan name except
  **Mcfly Analytics**.
