# Kill-shot matrix — September 2026

**Purpose:** Beat competitors on the failure merchants feel, not on feature count.

**Mcfly category:** Shopify marketing cash desk / spend affordability control. Action **Total ROAS = Shopify Total Sales after returns ÷ ad spend entered for the same period**. Net Sales is an optional view. Total ROAS and aMER are average portfolio measures, not marginal or causal channel returns.

**Current offer:** one full desk, **7-day trial then $39/store/month flat**. No forever-free plan, GMV tax, pixels, MTA, path credit, Mcfly-owned ad-platform OAuth, connector zoo, in-app MMM, or LLM operator.

## Price and evidence note

This is a **September 2026 positioning refresh**, not a live procurement quote. Competitor price shapes below come from Mcfly's public-market research last checked in **July 2026** and are marked approximate. Vendor pricing, GMV/ad-spend thresholds, annual terms, add-ons, and external charges can change; verify directly before publishing a dollar comparison. No review or install counts are used.

## What the matrix says

The six players cluster into three traps:

1. **Pipes without a decision** — SyncWith.
2. **Measurement/BI suites where MER is one contested tile** — Triple Whale, Northbeam, Polar, Kipify.
3. **Low-cost calculators without a durable operating ritual** — CashDash.

Mcfly should not claim that these products never show MER, profit, or blended performance. Many do. The opening is narrower and stronger: they do not make the **Shopify till cash close—defined, period-matched, break-even-aware, and cheap enough to keep—the product itself**.

## How Mcfly blows them out of the water without becoming them

1. **Own the definition.** Put Shopify Total Sales after returns ÷ same-period entered spend at the center; keep Net as a disclosed alternative and label average portfolio efficiency as noncausal.
2. **Win trusted time-to-value.** Make margin → real spend → trusted Total ROAS → next safe action achievable in under 10 minutes, with SAMPLE and incomplete periods impossible to mistake for live truth.
3. **Turn break-even into the operating rail.** A confirmed contribution margin should answer “can we afford this?” before any channel story begins.
4. **Make the next move constrained and auditable.** Hold, reduce, or step-test at the portfolio level with spend floors, input disclosure, and uncertainty—not fake channel profitability.
5. **Make CSV honesty feel premium.** Best-in-class export guidance, paste/import ergonomics, completeness checks, and optional merchant-paid pipe templates can beat brittle OAuth theater without creating a connector zoo.
6. **Charge the honest bill.** Keep the complete desk at $39/store/month after a 7-day trial; never punish GMV growth or bait merchants with a crippled forever-free tier.
7. **Steal the habit seat, not the feature sheet.** Polish Overview, Allocation, Goals, LTV/Acquisition, and Share Overview until Mcfly is the desk reopened for the cash decision; let suites remain optional CYA path decks.
