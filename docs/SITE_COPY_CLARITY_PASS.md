# Site copy clarity pass (2026-07-28)

**Audience:** Shopify ecommerce owners — solo operators through multi-brand.  
**Voice SoT:** [`MDS_RESEARCH_ABSORB.md`](./MDS_RESEARCH_ABSORB.md)  
**Goal:** Every page answers — What is this? What do I do Monday? Why trust it? — in plain English. Advanced Marketing Data Science shows up as *useful moves*, not jargon.

## Rules for this pass

1. **One job per section** — headline + ≤2 short sentences + optional proof/link.
2. **Feature → outcome** — “Confirm margin” → “sets your break-even floor,” not “unlock contribution intelligence.”
3. **Ban list** — unlock, seamless, leverage, empower, cutting-edge, game-changer, suite-grade, robust, harness, elevate, transform, delve, landscape, theater (customer copy).
4. **MDS noun** — thesis: **Advanced Marketing Data Science, made easy** — at most once per page (eyebrow or H1 support), never every lede. Body copy explains Total ROAS / break-even / Monday call in plain English.
5. **Large and small stores** — same math; never “enterprise-only” or “for startups only.”
6. **Honest CTAs** — Partner invite / App Store when listed / try calculator.
