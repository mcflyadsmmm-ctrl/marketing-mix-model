# Mcfly Analytics Strategy

## Target problem

Shopify shows sales. Ads Manager shows its own ROAS. Neither place holds **every dollar you actually spent** — Meta, Google, TikTok, **and billboards** — next to Shopify numbers, LTV Shopify does not compute, and a goals board you can run the year on. Operators already know the spreadsheet workaround. They uninstall apps that preach at them or leave Spend blank until they do homework.

## Our approach

Be the easy place to put **exact spend from any platform** (upload, paste, typed extras like billboards, later optional connects) beside **Shopify metrics**, **deeper LTV**, and **Goals**. Do not sell anti-pixel identity. Do not pretend we are Triple Whale or TrueProfit. Sales ÷ spend is a number on the board, not a religion.

## Who it's for

**Primary:** Shopify operator (founder or media buyer) — They're hiring Mcfly to see all spend next to store sales, understand payback/LTV Shopify Analytics lacks, and set yearly goals in one Admin app.

**Secondary:** Same person after they trust the numbers — They're hiring a $15–40K custom desk only when the stack is weirder than a public app.

## Key metrics

- **Listed** - App Store URL is a live listing, not 404 (Partner + apps.shopify.com)
- **Trusted first number** - Install → spend in (any method) → Shopify sales beside it, same dates, under 10 minutes
- **Week-1 retained** - Still installed with live spend on Your store (not Practice-only)
- **Trial conversion** - 7-day full-access trial → $39 because the desk earns the seat, never because a feature was withheld. One plan, no tiers.
- **Reviews** - First 15 honest App Store reviews from people who used live spend

## Tracks

### List and look like one product

Get through Partner review, match Pricing/site/in-app, one name (Mcfly Analytics), SAMPLE off for reviewers.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Nothing else exists until merchants can install from the Store.

### Spend in, any platform, almost no homework

Make upload/paste/typed extras (billboards included) the proud path. Every named platform and typed extra is included in the one plan. Optional connects later — never required for the core job.

_Why it serves the approach:_ This is the gap vs Shopify native and vs “pixel or go home.”

### Show the equation, the limits, and the price rules

Paint Shopify sales ÷ spend you added on Overview. Say it is not platform ROAS, not net profit, not which ad. In-app: Pro is a flat store fee, not a percent of sales or a per-order fee; uninstall/Free stops the next billing cycle.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Merchants leave modeled dashboards and GMV taxes when they can see how the number is built.

### LTV and Goals Shopify does not give you

Same period language everywhere. Real per-customer LTV/payback on Pro. A year Goals board that is obviously worth $39.

_Why it serves the approach:_ That is why someone pays instead of staying on a sheet.

### Distribution without a review moat fantasy

Design-partner installs, founder replies to every review, custom work as an upsell from a listed app — not a competing waitlist brand.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Zero reviews in a 200-app aisle is a product that never gets tried.

## Milestones

- **List live** - `https://apps.shopify.com/mcfly-analytics` is not 404
- **First Pro charge** - a real store pays $39 for LTV + Goals
- **15 reviews** - enough that search is no longer a brick wall

## Not working on

- Pixels, multi-touch attribution, “true ROAS,” or beating Triple Whale/Northbeam at their job
- Full COGS / SKU P&L / Amazon (TrueProfit/BeProfit already own that)
- Level 2 customer PII (name/email/phone) on first listing
- Hiding named extras or billboards behind Pro
- Anti-pixel marketing copy

## Marketing

**One-liner:** Upload spend from every platform — even billboards — next to Shopify sales, LTV, and goals.

**Key message:** Shopify does not ingest Meta/Google/offline cost. Mcfly does, without making you join an attribution religion. One plan: 7-day full-access trial, then $39/store/mo for the whole desk — spend, Total ROAS, LTV, and the full Goals board.
