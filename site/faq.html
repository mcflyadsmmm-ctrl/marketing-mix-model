Answers

What founders ask Monday.

  1. What is Total ROAS?

    Total ROAS is sales ÷ ad spend for the same period. It is simple cash math — money in over money out — not a path model. Glossary: Total ROAS definitions.

  2. How is Total ROAS different from platform ROAS?

    Platform ROAS credits clicks and paths inside an ad account. Total ROAS ignores who “won” the click and asks whether total sales cleared total spend. Same pipes; different question — see Product.

  3. Is Mcfly free now, and what about ~$79 later?

    The Shopify App Store listing is Free while we launch. Later: a flat ~$79/store/mo via Shopify Billing — announced before anything charges. Not a GMV tax. See Pricing.

  4. How do I enter ad spend?

    Paste Meta + Google from Sheets (day + amount) into the Spend tab — CSV file upload is secondary. Other platforms optional after. Sales stay on Shopify. Day one stays paste-first — no connector tax; flat fee later ~$79. Mcfly does not invent spend from pixels. Deep dive: paste-first spend.

  5. Do I need to connect Meta or Google accounts?

    No. Total ROAS does not require Meta or Google ad logins — and there is no “coming soon Meta sync” as the day-one path. Paste from Sheets is the easy path.

  6. Does Mcfly ship pixels or path attribution?

    No. No tracking pixels as a Mcfly product, no multi-touch / view-through “true ROAS,” no identity-graph cosplay. Why the graph broke: Why pixels fail.

  7. Why does Mcfly read numberOfOrders (PCD)?

    For new vs returning counts we use Protected Customer Data minimally: opaque customer id + numberOfOrders only — no names, emails, or addresses, and no customer CRM. Order totals power Total ROAS; see Privacy for the full diet.

  8. How do I install — Partner invite or type my shop domain?

    App Store Free is primary for Mcfly Analytics. Prefer email? Request a Partner invite via the waitlist. We do not offer a public “type your .myshopify.com” install form on the marketing site. Start at Support.

  9. What is break-even Total ROAS?

    Break-even Total ROAS comes from your contribution margin — the efficiency floor where ads stop losing money. Operate Total ROAS above that floor, then make one allocation call. Full desk: Product.
