Objections

Product questions.

  1. What’s in the Shopify desk?

    Five analysis tabs plus Settings: Overview · Orders · Customers · Spend · Goals. Growth and LTV are Customers chips, not top tabs. Overview, Orders, and Customers work from the orders you already have — Spend is optional and last. Last-refreshed is always clear. Try: full Snowdevil demo.

  2. What does Mcfly show that Analytics does not?

    Typical order is the leftover: Shopify AOV is the mean; there is no native median ticket. One wholesale PO moves Shopify AOV; typical does not. Returning dollars, weekend mix, days-to-second, and LTV 30/90/365 are not a native hole: ShopifyQL can show returning sales $; Shopify Reports can Group by day of week; native RFM shows average days since last order; Shopify already has customer cohort reports. Mcfly LTV 30/90/365 is observed orders on this desk. Repeat Customer Insights from $59 is the closer LTV and purchase-latency app. Empty spend paints —, never 0×. Add spend last when you want Total ROAS vs break-even. Try: the demo.

  3. What does it cost?

    One plan: 7-day trial, then $39/store/mo for the whole desk. Trial is 90 days of orders; paid is up to 24 months. No Free plan. Not a GMV tax. Copy or Save PNG is the seat — Basic Shopify has none. Uninstall stops the charge. See Pricing.

  4. How does spend get in?

    Type a day on Spend (amount + date + channel, or a named extra like billboards) or paste/upload a CSV. Every platform including billboards. No ad OAuth. Spend is optional, last. Guide: spend ingest.

  5. What is Total ROAS?

    Total ROAS = Shopify Total Sales ÷ entered spend for the same window. Average portfolio efficiency — not “true ROAS,” not MTA. Empty spend is —. See Pricing.

  6. What is break-even Total ROAS?

    Break-even ≈ 1 ÷ contribution margin — the floor where ads stop losing money on average. Stay above it, then change spend on Spend. Not a parked calculator. Formula: Product.

  7. How do I install?

    Install from the Shopify App Store. Steps on Support. There is no “type your .myshopify.com” form on this site (including mcflyads.com).

  8. Do you do MMM, pixels, or multi-touch attribution?

    No. Mcfly Analytics does not ship tracking pixels, MTA, path/view-through credit, or “true ROAS.” We measure sales ÷ entered spend.

  9. Is typical order the same as Shopify AOV?

    No. Typical order is the median ticket. Shopify AOV is the mean. One wholesale order moves AOV; typical order does not.

  10. Can I share this with my bookkeeper or agency without a staff account?

    You copy or Save PNG. Founder: this month vs last year, typical order, returning dollars — Slack or WhatsApp. Books: the same lines in email. Agency: the year board as PNG. Mcfly does not email or Slack. Grow+ staff can be Dashboards only (Overview + Live view), not full Admin. Reports permission is every report, not one report. Basic Shopify has no staff seat.

  11. Is this a Triple Whale replacement?

    No. No pixel, no MTA. Repeat Customer Insights from $59 is the closer LTV / latency app. $39 flat for order-history numbers plus optional typed spend. If you need their pixel, stay there. $39 stays $39 at $5M.

  12. Is this Repeat Customer Insights?

    No. Repeat Customer Insights from $59 does LTV, purchase latency, RFM, and tagging. Better Reports from $19.90 exports and schedules Sheets. Mcfly is one Admin desk: median ticket, returning dollars, and month/quarter/year on the same board. $39 stays $39 at $5M.

  13. Do I export Orders CSV?

    No. Mcfly reads orders in Admin. Shopify Admin can export Orders CSV; we don’t make you. CSV discount and refund columns are easy to double-count.

  14. Do I have to enter COGS before I see numbers?

    No. Typical order and returning dollars paint from orders you already have. Lifetimely and TrueProfit need costs for net profit. Mcfly does not ingest COGS and does not paint net profit.
