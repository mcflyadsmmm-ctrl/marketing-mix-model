FAQ

Shopify desk questions.

Deeper order numbers first. Total ROAS = Shopify Total Sales ÷ ad spend when you add spend. Break-even ≈ 1/margin. 7-day trial, then $39/store/mo. No pixels. No path credit.

What do I get without spend? Typical order, weekends, returning sales, days to a second order, LTV from orders — About 60 days of orders on a new install.
What is Total ROAS? Shopify Total Sales ÷ total logged ad spend for the same period. Portfolio efficiency — not platform ROAS, not causal “true ROAS.”
What is break-even Total ROAS? ≈ 1 ÷ contribution margin — the floor where ads stop losing money on average.
What’s in the desk? Overview, Spend, Goals, LTV / Acquisition. Whole desk on one plan after trial.
What does it cost? 7-day full-access trial, then $39/store/mo. Uninstall stops the charge. Not a GMV tax.
How does spend get in? Type a day or period, or paste/upload an Ads Manager CSV. No ad OAuth required day one.
Pixels / MTA / true ROAS? No. We do not ship pixels, multi-touch attribution, or path credit.
How do I install? Only from the Shopify App Store. There is no “type your .myshopify.com” form on this site.
