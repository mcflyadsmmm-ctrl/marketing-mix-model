FAQ

Short answers.

The desk, the SAMPLE, and the plan. Open a question. The sample desk is the place to click the real controls.

What opens first?

Orders. This month’s sales, then typical order and returning customers, with the daily chart under them.

What are the periods?

This month, Last month, This quarter, This year, Last 12 months. The frame stays. The stored window changes.

What if I have not entered spend?

Sales ÷ spend stays blank. The desk does not fill in a ratio.

What is a goal?

One sales target you save. The desk does not compare sales to it before that save.

Is the sample a real shop?

No. SAMPLE · Northline Supply uses figures already published on this site. It is not a client result and not the shop that installs the app.

What does it cost?

7-day trial, then $39 per store per month, flat. Trial and paid share the full desk and up to 24 months of orders. Uninstall stops the next cycle. A period already paid may run to its end.

Pixels?

No ads. No pixels, path credit, or multi-touch.
