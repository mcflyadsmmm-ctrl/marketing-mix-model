Product questions.

  1. What is Total ROAS?

    Total ROAS = Shopify Total Sales ÷ total logged ad spend for the same period. Average portfolio efficiency — not marginal channel return, not causal “true ROAS.” Glossary: Total ROAS glossary. Run it: ROAS calculator.

  2. What is break-even Total ROAS?

    Break-even ≈ 1 ÷ contribution margin — the floor where ads stop losing money on average. Tool: break-even calculator. Full desk: Product.

  3. What’s in the Shopify desk?

    Spend, Overview, LTV, Goals — whole desk. Sample data or Live data. Try: Sample desk.

  4. What does Mcfly cost?

    One plan: 7-day full-access trial, then $39/store/mo. Uninstall stops the charge. See Pricing.

  5. How does spend get in?

    Type one day on Spend (amount + date + channel) or paste/upload a CSV. No ad OAuth required day one. Guide: spend ingest.

  6. How do I install?

    Until listing is live: Partner review + /demo + /pricing. Steps on Support. No “type your .myshopify.com” form on this site.

  7. Do you do MMM, pixels, or multi-touch attribution?

    No. No tracking pixels, MTA, path/view-through credit, or “true ROAS.” We measure sales ÷ spend.
