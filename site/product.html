The math

One formula. Two numbers. No black box.

Cash MER = Shopify sales (period) ÷ ad spend (same period)

Break-even MER ≈ 1 ÷ contribution margin

Operate above break-even. Shift mix to protect it. This is the math a CFO would sign off on — no path credit anywhere in it.

The Monday ritual

Ten minutes.
Same three moves, every week.

No dashboard safari, no “which touchpoint won.” Pull sales, pull spend, read one number against break-even, make one call.

  1. 01

    Sales from the till

    Shopify Admin for the period you care about — orders, net sales, refunds netted out. Real store revenue, not platform-claimed revenue.

  2. 02

    Spend is money out

    Meta + Google — manual or CSV day one, live pipes as we harden the connectors. Whatever actually left the bank for ads, nothing “influenced.”

  3. 03

    MER vs break-even

    Divide. Compare to your break-even from contribution margin. One allocation card — rules-based, inputs shown, no mystery model behind it.

Know your floor

Break-even isn’t a vibe.
It’s 1 ÷ margin.

Contribution margin sets the ceiling on how much MER you can spend down before ads stop making cash and start burning it.

Say your margin runs 35% — break-even MER ≈ 2.86×. This week’s cash MER comes in at 3.2×: above break-even, protect the mix.

Comes in at 2.4× instead: you’re buying revenue at a loss before margin even lands — even while the platform dashboard still claims 4×. That gap is the whole product.
