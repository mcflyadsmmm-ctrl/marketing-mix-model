Mcfly Analytics

Cash MER. Live spend. Allocation you can audit.

One brain for spend-vs-sales. Shopify-native first; Sheets when you want the grid. No pixels. No path credit.

What you get

  • Shopify sales in — from Admin API, for the period you care about.
  • Ad spend out — Meta + Google daily (manual/CSV on day one; live pipes next).
  • MER + break-even MER — from your contribution margin, not a black-box model.
  • Channel mix — what you spent where, against total sales.
  • One allocation card — rules-based suggestion with inputs shown. Not “who won the click.”
  • Freshness — last synced, recon hints when connectors fail.

Surfaces (build order)

  1. This marketing site
  2. Shopify embedded app (primary product)
  3. Google Sheets companion (same numbers)

What we will not ship

  • Multi-touch / path attribution
  • View-through and pixel causality cosplay
  • A 40-connector data warehouse dressed as SyncWith
  • Custom MMM consulting as the core offer

Who it’s for

Shopify operators and lean agencies who already know platform ROAS doesn’t equal P&L — and want a weekly source of truth they can trust.
