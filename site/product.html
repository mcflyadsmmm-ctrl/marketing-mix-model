When you add spend

Break-even is 1 ÷ margin.
Not a 40% pledge.

If you type spend, contribution margin sets how far Total ROAS can fall before ads stop making cash. You type the margin in Settings. Mcfly does not promise a 40% floor.

Contribution margin against break-even Total ROAS
Contribution margin Break-even Total ROAS
25%4.00×
35%2.86×
45%2.22×
55%1.82×

Margin at 35% → break-even ≈ 2.86×. A week at 3.2×: above that shop’s floor — protect the mix.

At 2.4×: you’re buying revenue at a loss for that margin — even if a channel dashboard still looks fine. That gap is optional Spend math, not the Overview greeting.
