Know your floor

Break-even isn’t a vibe.
It’s 1 ÷ margin.

Contribution margin sets how far Total ROAS can fall before ads stop making cash and start burning it.

Contribution margin against break-even Total ROAS
Contribution margin Break-even Total ROAS
25%4.00×
35%2.86×
45%2.22×
55%1.82×

Margin at 35% → break-even ≈ 2.86×. This week at 3.2×: above break-even — protect the mix.

At 2.4×: you’re buying revenue at a loss — even if a channel dashboard still looks fine. That gap is the product.
