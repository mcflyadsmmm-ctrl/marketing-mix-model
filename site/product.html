Know your floor

Break-even isn’t a vibe.
It’s 1 ÷ margin.

Contribution margin sets the ceiling on how much Total ROAS you can spend down before ads stop making cash and start burning it.

Contribution margin against break-even Total ROAS
Contribution margin Break-even Total ROAS
25%4.00×
35%2.86×
45%2.22×
55%1.82×

Say your margin runs 35% — break-even Total ROAS ≈ 2.86×. This week’s Total ROAS comes in at 3.2×: above break-even, protect the mix.

Comes in at 2.4× instead: you’re buying revenue at a loss before margin even lands — even if a channel dashboard still looks healthy. That gap is the whole product.
