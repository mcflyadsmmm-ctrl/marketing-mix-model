Four steps from first call to ownership transfer. Same spine every build — discovery, fixed fee, demos, handoff.

Discovery call

Fit, data access, success metric. We say no when pixels, MTA, or forensic depth is what you actually need.

Fixed-fee SOW

Written engagement letter: tier, fee, deliverables, refuse list. No hourly surprise. See the specimen.

Build with weekly demos

Metric contracts first. Then desk or memo work with a standing weekly touch on Tier II / III.

Handoff & ownership

Deliverables in your environment, runbooks, time-boxed credentials. Retainer optional — never required to keep the system.

Pricing & fit FAQs

Answers before the call

Where do market comps land?

Discrete analytics / decision-desk projects often run $5–40K; dashboard builds $5–20K; full MMM projects often $30–150K; forensic paid-media audits start near $100K. Mcfly Analytics — Custom Data Solutions publishes the $5–25K band so the SOW isn’t a surprise.

Is $5–8K a “real” audit?

Yes for a finance-grade close memo: invoice vs platform spend, variance flags, and a sales/outcome period check. That sits with elite boutique audits (~$6K class). It is not a $100K forensic agency/rebate audit — and not a $249 CSV micro-recon. Different jobs; we name ours.

Do you replace Recast, Measured, Paramark, or Nielsen?

No. We build the weekly decision desk and metric contracts. Always-on measurement OS pricing often starts near $100K/year — we don’t fake that inside $25K. If you need full MMM SaaS later, we say so.

Who owns the data and the system?

You. Work lands in your environment when possible. Credentials are time-boxed. Handoff includes runbooks. Retainer is optional continuity — not a hostage fee.

What blows the budget?

Dirty source data and undefined metrics. Cleanup and scope expansion only via written addendum. Tools and third-party licenses are billed at cost, called out in the SOW.

Why refuse pixels and “true ROAS”?

Identity graphs thinned; finance still needs sales (or qualified pipeline) ÷ audited spend. Path credit is a different product category — we don’t sell it.

How is this different from the Shopify app?

The Shopify cash desk is a productized Total ROAS seat for merchants. Mcfly Analytics — Custom Data Solutions is a fixed-fee practice for companies that need spend recon, lead-gen desks, or MDS beyond that app — toggle between the two on this site.
