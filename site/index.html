Why this exists

Platforms sell identity graphs.
Browsers deleted them.

A “true ROAS” model needs one continuous person across ad, site, and checkout. Privacy cut that rail years ago. Safari ITP squeezed JS cookies to ~a week — ~a day after an ad-click landing. iOS strips the click IDs off tapped links. CNAME cloaking collapsed. When the graph goes sparse, the model still prints neat credit — stacked “revenue” that never reconciles to the till.

  • ITP: JS cookies ~a week, ~a day post ad-click
  • iOS LTP strips click IDs from links
  • CNAME cloaking collapsed on IP distance
  • “Triple Pixel” = JS + UTMs + cookies, not magic
  • Junk into CAPI → delivery optimizes to noise

Mcfly skips the graph entirely: sales ÷ spend, straight off the till. Path credit trains the model. Cash MER trains Monday’s budget.
