    Why this exists

    Identity graphs thinned.
    Bank math still works.

    Path models need one continuous person across ad, site, and checkout. Privacy cut that rail years ago — Safari ITP, iOS link stripping, CNAME caps. When the graph goes sparse, credit can still look neat while stacked “revenue” drifts from your real sales. Start where the bank statement starts.

    • ITP: JS cookies ~a week, ~a day post ad-click
    • iOS LTP strips click IDs from links
    • CNAME cloaking collapsed on IP distance
    • Pixel stacks ≠ sales reconciliation
    • Junk into CAPI → delivery optimizes to noise

    Mcfly skips the graph: Total ROAS = sales ÷ spend. That trains Monday’s budget — not a path model.
