Before you install

What a merchant asks before Install.

What does this show that Shopify Analytics Overview does not?

Typical order (median, not AOV). Overview’s returning number is a headcount rate; ShopifyQL can show returning sales $. Weekend mix as a % of this window — Reports can already Group by day of week. Days-to-second. LTV 30/90/365 from orders you already have. Overview can already compare one range to last year — Mcfly keeps month, quarter, and year on one board. Repeat Customer Insights from $59 is the closer LTV / latency app if you want RFM and tagging.

Does $39 climb with GMV or monthly orders?

No. $39 stays $39 at $5M. Polar’s App Store list price starts at $750/mo and moves with GMV. Lifetimely lists $149/mo at 3,000 orders. TrueProfit lists from $35 plus a per-order surcharge. Repeat Customer Insights lists from $59/mo. Better Reports lists from $19.90.

Why are there 0 reviews?

The Shopify listing is live as mcfly-analytics-public. Reviews: 0. We do not invent a 4.9 or an install count. Polar lists 4.9 (117). Triple Whale lists 4.1 (91). Lifetimely lists 4.9 (538). TrueProfit lists 4.9 (898). Better Reports lists 5.0 (1,199). Repeat Customer Insights lists 5.0 (14). Install reads your shop. The live App Store card still leads with ad spend next to store sales.

What does the 7-day trial include?

90 days of order history. Paid is up to 24 months. Then $39/store/mo. Uninstall stops the charge. No Free plan.

Is this Triple Whale, Polar, or Repeat Customer Insights?

No. Triple Whale listing is Free / Foundation $219 / Automate $749. Polar is from $750 GMV-based. Repeat Customer Insights from $59 does LTV, purchase latency, RFM, and tagging. Mcfly is one Shopify desk. Overview · Orders · Customers work at $0 spend. Spend is last and optional. $39 stays $39 at $5M / not a GMV tax.

Do you put a pixel on my storefront?

No. No Mcfly pixel, no theme lock-in, no Meta/Google/TikTok login, no path credit, no “true ROAS.” Total ROAS is Shopify Total Sales ÷ spend you type. Empty spend paints —. Full FAQ.

Do I have to enter COGS before I see numbers?

No. Typical order and returning dollars paint from orders you already have. Lifetimely and TrueProfit need costs for net profit. Mcfly does not ingest COGS and does not paint net profit.

Can finance or an agency see this without a staff seat?

You copy or Save PNG. Mcfly never posts to Slack, never sends mail, and does not write to a project board. Shopify staff with Analytics see every report, not one card. Polar and Lifetimely send Slack or mail; we do not.

Are these numbers a real store?

No. Snowdevil is SAMPLE, not a live client. Install reads your shop. The public desk is /demo.
