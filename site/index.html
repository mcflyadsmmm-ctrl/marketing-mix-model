The problem

Platform reports and the books often tell different stories.

Ad platforms credit journeys. Finance records money. Those two views can both be internally consistent and still disagree.

Double counting

Several channels can claim the same order. Add those claims together and you invent a profit-and-loss that never existed.

Path models

Multi-touch attribution produces a tidy journey. Real buying is messier. Optimizing the model is not the same as protecting cash.

What we will not sell
