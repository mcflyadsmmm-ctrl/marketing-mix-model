Mcfly vs profit trackers — different Monday job

Profit apps (TrueProfit, Lifetimely, BeProfit, and peers) answer “what’s my net P&L?” Mcfly answers “did ad spend clear break-even — and what’s one budget move?” Both can matter. Not the same desk.

Keep your profit tracker if you need it. Use it for COGS and full contribution P&L. Mcfly owns the Total ROAS Export memo: Shopify sales after returns ÷ spend against break-even from a margin input.

Decision tree

When a profit tracker is right

  • You need SKU-level COGS, shipping, and fee truth before you trust profit margin.
  • Finance already lives in a full P&L dashboard and only wants ads as one line.
  • You’re optimizing inventory and contribution profit — not the weekly ad-spend close.

When Mcfly is right

  • You already know approximate profit margin and need Total ROAS vs break-even this Monday.
  • You’re tired of Sheets dividing sales ÷ spend by hand and want a save/export memo.
  • You want a flat-fee cash desk that sits next to suites or profit apps — not another OS.
