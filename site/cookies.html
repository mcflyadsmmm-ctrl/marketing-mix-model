Cookies & similar tech

Last updated: July 28, 2026. Short notice — details in Privacy.

We do not run marketing analytics pixels, Meta Pixel, Google Ads conversion tags, or path-attribution scripts on mcflyads.com.

What may load

  • Cloudflare — essential CDN/hosting cookies or bot-management signals for site delivery and security.
  • Self-hosted fonts — woff2 files from /assets/fonts-local.css on this origin. The marketing site does not load Google’s font CDN.
  • Local storage — optional UI prefs in the public SAMPLE demo or similar (not used to sell ads).
  • Support mail — when you email support, we process the fields you send (see Privacy). That is mail, not a tracking cookie.

Shopify app

The embedded Mcfly Analytics app runs inside Shopify Admin. Sessions and cookies there are governed by Shopify plus our OAuth session storage described in Privacy.

Choices

You can block third-party connections in your browser; fonts then fall back to the system stack. To delete support email contact data, email mcflyadsmmm@gmail.com.
