Mcfly Analytics

Privacy

Last updated: July 21, 2026. This is a working policy for early access; we will harden it before App Store launch.

Who we are

Mcfly Analytics (“Mcfly,” “we”) provides a marketing efficiency product for Shopify merchants. Contact: mcflyadsmmm@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account and contact details you provide (name, email, store domain).
  • Shopify store data required for MER (e.g. order / sales totals for selected periods).
  • Ad platform spend and account metadata when you connect Meta, Google, or paste spend manually.
  • Technical logs (IP, user agent, error events) for reliability and security.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your store data.
  • We do not use path-attribution pixels as a product surface.
  • We do not train public AI models on your merchant data.

Why we process data

To provide MER dashboards, spend sync, allocation suggestions, billing, support, and to meet legal obligations.

Retention

We retain account and metrics data while your subscription is active and for a reasonable period afterward for disputes, backups, and legal requirements. You may request deletion subject to those needs.

Processors

We use infrastructure and SaaS vendors (hosting, database, email, error monitoring, Shopify Billing). They process data only to provide services to us.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data, or object to certain processing. Email us to exercise them.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
