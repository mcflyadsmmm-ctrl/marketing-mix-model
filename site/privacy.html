Plain-English summary

  • We read Shopify order totals and order counts, classify unique customers as new vs returning using only opaque customer IDs and lifetime order counts (no names, emails, or addresses), and combine that with the ad spend you enter — that is the data diet behind cash MER, break-even, and allocation.
  • We don't sell merchant or customer data, run tracking pixels, or build a customer CRM from your orders.
  • Uninstalling triggers Shopify's shop/redact webhook — we delete your Mcfly shop record, settings, and spend entries on our side.
  • Same cash MER religion as the product: we measure money against money, not who "won" the click. The Shopify App Store listing is free while we launch; see Pricing for the later flat ~$79 path via Shopify Billing.

This summary is an orientation, not a substitute — the full policy below governs.

Who we are

Mcfly Ads (“Mcfly,” “we”) is operated by Marty Smithson (Utah, United States). Contact: mcflyadsmmm@gmail.com. Support: mcflyads.com/support.

This policy applies to mcflyads.com and the Mcfly Ads Shopify app.

Why we process data

To run the MER dashboard, break-even MER, and rules-based allocation; to provide support and security; and to comply with law and Shopify’s app requirements (including GDPR webhooks). We measure money spent on ads against money Shopify recorded as sales — not who “won” the click.

What we collect

  • Account contact you give us (name, email, store URL) via waitlist or support.
  • Shopify shop domain and OAuth session tokens when you install the app.
  • Order / sales totals and order counts for periods you view (Shopify Admin API read_orders) to compute cash MER and AOV.
  • New vs returning customer counts derived in-request from opaque Shopify customer IDs and numberOfOrders only. We do not request or store customer name, email, phone, or address, and we do not keep a customer CRM.
  • Ad spend you enter (CSV or manual: Meta, Google, Microsoft, TikTok, Affiliate, Email, Other) and settings (margin %, target MER).
  • Technical logs (IP, user agent, error events) for security and reliability.

What we refuse to do

  • We do not sell merchant or customer data.
  • We do not run path-attribution pixels as a product.
  • We do not build a customer CRM from your orders — we aggregate sales for MER.
  • We do not train public AI models on your store data.

Shopify GDPR / data requests

When Shopify sends customers/data_request, customers/redact, or shop/redact, we process them per Shopify’s timelines. Shop uninstall and shop/redact delete your Mcfly shop record, settings, spend entries, and sessions.

Retention

We keep app data while the app is installed. After uninstall / shop redact, we delete merchant app data subject to short backup windows and legal holds.

Processors

We use infrastructure vendors such as Cloudflare (site hosting/CDN), our application host and database provider (when the Shopify app is live), email, and Shopify. They process data only to provide services to us.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, or export of personal data. Email mcflyadsmmm@gmail.com. Merchants can also uninstall the app to stop processing store data through Mcfly.

Governing law

Privacy disputes relating to this site and app are handled under Utah, USA law, consistent with our Terms.

Children

The service is for businesses, not directed at children under 16.

Changes
