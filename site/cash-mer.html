Total ROAS glossary

Total ROAS is sales ÷ ad spend — then break-even, then one budget move. Deeper product story on Product; common questions on FAQ.

Definitions

Five terms.
One religion.

  • Total ROAS

    sales divided by ad spend for the same window — money in over money out. Not attributed revenue. Not path credit. Not “true ROAS.”

    Total ROAS = sales ÷ ad spend

  • Break-even Total ROAS

    The efficiency floor from your contribution margin — below it, ads lose money even if a platform ROAS looks healthy. You set margin; the desk computes the floor.

    break-even Total ROAS ≈ 1 ÷ contribution margin

  • Allocation from cash

    A rules-based shift suggestion from channel efficiency versus break-even — auditable inputs, one call. We do not re-rank creatives by multi-touch credit.

    if Total ROAS < break-even → protect / shift mix

  • Monday ritual

    Spend in. Sales in. Total ROAS vs break-even. One allocation call. Done. Weekly cadence — not a live path dashboard.

    margin → spend → Total ROAS → one budget move

  • Spend CSV (how money out gets in)

    No ad OAuth required. Export daily cost from Meta, Google, Microsoft, TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat, Reddit, X, LinkedIn, Amazon, Apple Search, Impact/CJ, Klaviyo/Mailchimp, or Other → upload → Mcfly combines. Covers channels that never get a clean Shopify connector. Full list and flow: Product · spend spine.

    export → upload → combine (sales stay on Shopify)
