Payout vs UI

Payout is not Ads Manager revenue

A Shopify payout is money that hit the bank after fees and reserve timing. Ads Manager “revenue” is a UI column. They are two totals.

Fees, refunds, and payout delay move the bank number. Delivery windows move the spend invoice. Do not subtract UI revenue from UI spend and call it a close.
