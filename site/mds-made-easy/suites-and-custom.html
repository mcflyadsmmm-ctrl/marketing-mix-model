Suites, Custom, and when to stop DIY

You do not need to delete Triple Whale to run a till desk. You need one number finance trusts.

Coexist. Suites own CYA path decks. Mcfly owns sales ÷ spend, break-even, and the Monday move. Custom is for desks that do not fit the Shopify App Store shape.

Keep the suite when…

  • Stakeholders still want path narratives for politics.
  • You already pay for it and it is not the Monday bottleneck.

Lean on the Free / Pro desk when…

  • You need period-aligned Total ROAS in minutes.
  • CSV spend is enough (or typed extras like billboards).
  • You want a flat fee later — not a GMV tax.

Buy Custom Data Solutions when…

  • Non-Shopify or multi-system books.
  • You need written metric contracts and a weekly decision desk ($5–25K bands).
  • DIY CSV + AI prompts are not enough governance.
