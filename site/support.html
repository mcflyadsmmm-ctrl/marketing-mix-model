What Total ROAS means here

Total ROAS = Shopify sales after returns ÷ ad spend for the same period. Break-even ≈ 1 ÷ profit margin. Platform ROAS can disagree with Shopify sales — that’s expected, not a sync bug. Platforms credit clicks and views; Mcfly counts sales after returns against money spent on ads.

Common questions

Why no “type your .myshopify.com” install on this site?

Shopify requires App Store (or Partner Dashboard) install. We never put a public shop-domain form on mcflyads.com. Install Free from the App Store; email Support if you need a hand.

What’s Free vs Pro?

Free = Meta + Google spend (CSV / Connections when ready), Total ROAS desk, break-even, basic allocation. Pro ($39/store/mo) unlocks Customer LTV, every spend channel, and advanced Goals / Export memo. Listing Pricing stays Free until Billing is announced — see Pricing.

Why isn’t it free forever?

App Store listing is Free while we launch — not a bait price. Target paid is $39/store/mo flat; we tell you the date and terms before anything bills.

Do you use pixels or track my customers?

No. Mcfly is not a pixel, multi-touch, or “true ROAS” product. We read Shopify order totals, classify new vs returning with opaque customer id + numberOfOrders only (no name/email/address), and the ad spend you enter. That’s the whole data diet.

How do I get spend into Mcfly with Google Sheets?

In Spend: pick platforms → Download blank CSV or Open Google Sheets → File → Import → Upload → fill daily amounts → File → Download → CSV → paste on Spend. Optional: map SyncWith / Coupler / Supermetrics (you pay those tools). Guide: Sheets spend guide. Free path never requires SyncWith.

Do I connect Meta or Google to Mcfly?

No for day one. Paste Meta + Google from Sheets (CSV secondary) — no ad OAuth, no pixels. Optional: fill Mcfly’s pipe template with SyncWith / Coupler / Supermetrics / Coefficient (you pay those tools), then import the CSV. Not a “Works with” partnership. Overview: paste-first spend.

Can I automate spend without connecting every ad account?

Yes. Free path is always paste/CSV. If you already pay SyncWith, Coupler, Supermetrics, or Coefficient, point them at Mcfly’s date,channel,amount template, download CSV, import on Spend. Examples: long · wide.

My Total ROAS doesn’t match Meta or Google. Is something broken?

Almost always no. Platforms credit conversions to clicks and views; that can run ahead of banked sales. Total ROAS only counts Shopify sales after returns ÷ ad spend for the same period. Send a screenshot with the period if you want a second look.

What happens to my data if I uninstall?

Shopify sends a shop-redact webhook; we delete your Mcfly shop record, settings, and spend entries, subject to short backup windows. Detail: Privacy.

How fast will I hear back?

Usually the same business day. Small team, real inbox — if it’s urgent before a Monday budget call, say so in the subject line.

What we refuse to build

No pixels. No MTA. No “true ROAS.” If a request needs path attribution, the answer is no — that’s a product line, not a missing feature. We measure ad spend against Total Sales. Full stop.
