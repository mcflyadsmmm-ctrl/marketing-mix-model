What “cash MER” means here

MER = Shopify sales for a period ÷ ad spend for the same period. Break-even MER ≈ 1 ÷ contribution margin. Platform-reported ROAS can disagree with the till — that is expected, not a sync bug, because ad platforms count clicks and views their own model credits, not dollars that landed in your bank.

Common questions

Why no “type your .myshopify.com” install on this site?

Shopify requires App Store / Partner install flows. We never put a public shop-domain form on mcflyads.com — that path is insecure and against our religion. Install from the App Store or a Partner invite we send you.

Why isn't it free forever?

Design partners run the desk free while we harden it — that's a launch phase, not a bait price. Target paid is ~$79/store/mo flat, and we tell partners the date and terms before anything bills. No surprise invoice.

Do you use pixels or track my customers?

No. Mcfly is not a pixel, multi-touch attribution, or “true ROAS” product and never will be. We read Shopify order totals (read_orders), classify new vs returning with opaque customer id + numberOfOrders only (no name/email/address CRM), and the ad spend you enter — that’s the entire data diet.

My MER doesn't match what Meta or Google shows. Is something broken?

Almost always no. Ad platforms report conversions their own model credits to a click or view — that number can run well ahead of what actually landed in your bank. Cash MER only counts money Shopify recorded as sales against money that left for ads, same period, both sides. Send us a screenshot with the period if you want a second look.

What happens to my data if I uninstall?

Shopify sends us a shop-redact webhook and we delete your Mcfly shop record, settings, and spend entries on our side, subject to short backup windows. Full detail in Privacy.

How fast will I hear back?

Usually the same business day. We're a small team reading a real inbox, not routing you through tiers — if something's urgent (numbers look wrong before a Monday budget call), say so in the subject line.

What helps us help you

What we refuse to build

No pixels. No MTA. No “true ROAS.” If a request needs path attribution to work, the answer is no — that's religion, not a missing feature. We measure money spent on ads against money Shopify recorded as sales, full stop.
