What Total ROAS means here

Total ROAS = Shopify Total Sales ÷ ad spend for the same period. Break-even ≈ 1 ÷ profit margin. Platform ROAS can disagree with Shopify sales — that’s expected, not a sync bug. Platforms credit clicks and views; Mcfly counts Total Sales against money spent on ads.

Common questions

Why no “type your .myshopify.com” install on this site?

Shopify requires App Store (or Partner Dashboard) install. We never put a public shop-domain form on mcflyads.com. Until listing is live: Partner review + /demo + /pricing. Email Support if you need a hand.

What’s the price?

One plan: 7-day full-access trial, then $39/store/mo for the whole desk (Spend, Overview, LTV, Goals). Uninstall stops the charge. Billing is not a desk mode — desk modes are Sample data | Live data only. See Pricing.

Sample data vs Live data?

Sample data = example numbers so you can click around. Live data = this shop’s Shopify sales + the spend you upload from every platform you buy, including billboards. Never Free / Pro / trial as a view.

Do you use pixels or track my customers?

No. Mcfly is not a pixel, multi-touch, or “true ROAS” product. We read Shopify order totals, classify new vs returning with opaque customer id + numberOfOrders only (no name/email/address), and the ad spend you enter. That’s the whole data diet.

How do I get spend into Mcfly?

Three doors on Spend: (1) pick channels → download template, (2) type channel + amount + day/week/month/quarter/half-year/year — we show “That’s $X/day”, (3) Ads Manager CSV. Sheets path: download blank CSV or open Google Sheets → fill → download CSV → paste on Spend. Guide: Sheets spend guide.

Do I connect Meta or Google to Mcfly?

No. We already have Shopify sales/orders/LTV. You add spend by day by channel — no Meta/Google OAuth, no pixels. Optional: fill Mcfly’s pipe template with SyncWith / Coupler / Supermetrics / Coefficient (you pay those tools), then import the CSV. Overview: paste-first spend.

Can I automate spend without connecting every ad account?

Yes. Paste/CSV is always available. If you already pay SyncWith, Coupler, Supermetrics, or Coefficient, point them at Mcfly’s date,channel,amount template, download CSV, import on Spend. Examples: long · wide.

My Total ROAS doesn’t match Meta or Google. Is something broken?

Almost always no. Platforms credit conversions to clicks and views; that can run ahead of banked sales. Total ROAS only counts Shopify sales after returns ÷ ad spend for the same period. Send a screenshot with the period if you want a second look.

What happens to my data if I uninstall?

Shopify sends a shop-redact webhook; we delete your Mcfly shop record, settings, and spend entries, subject to short backup windows. Uninstall also stops the charge. Detail: Privacy.

How fast will I hear back?

Usually the same business day. Small team, real inbox — if it’s urgent before a Monday budget call, say so in the subject line.

What helps us help you

  • Shopify store domain
  • Period (MTD / QTD / YTD)
  • Spend source and amounts (template, typed period, or Ads Manager CSV)
  • Screenshot if numbers look wrong vs Ads Manager — same period both sides

What we refuse to build

No pixels. No MTA. No “true ROAS.” If a request needs path attribution, the answer is no — that’s a product line, not a missing feature. We measure ad spend against Total Sales. Full stop.
