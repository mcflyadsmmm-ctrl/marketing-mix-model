What “Total ROAS” means here

Total ROAS = sales for a period ÷ ad spend for the same period. Break-even Total ROAS ≈ 1 ÷ contribution margin. Platform-reported ROAS can disagree with your sales math — that is expected, not a sync bug, because ad platforms count clicks and views their own model credits, not dollars that landed in your bank.

Common questions

Why no “type your .myshopify.com” install on this site?

Shopify requires App Store / Partner install flows. We never put a public shop-domain form on mcflyads.com — that path is insecure and we refuse it. Primary: install free from the App Store. Secondary: a Partner invite from the waitlist.

Why isn't it free forever?

App Store listing is Free while we launch — that's a launch phase, not a bait price. Target paid is ~$79/store/mo flat, and we tell you the date and terms before anything bills. No surprise invoice.

Do you use pixels or track my customers?

No. Mcfly is not a pixel, multi-touch attribution, or “true ROAS” product and never will be. We read Shopify order totals (read_orders), classify new vs returning with opaque customer id + numberOfOrders only (no name/email/address CRM), and the ad spend you enter — that’s the entire data diet.

How do I get spend into Mcfly with Google Sheets?

In Spend: pick platforms → Download blank CSV (Admin-safe) or Open Google Sheets → in the new Sheet use File → Import → Upload your CSV → Replace spreadsheet → Import data → fill daily amounts (or optionally map SyncWith / Coupler / Supermetrics — you pay those tools) → File → Download → CSV → paste on Spend. Step-by-step: Sheets spend guide. Free path never requires SyncWith. No fake “Works with” logos.

Do I connect Meta or Google to Mcfly?

Free now: paste Meta + Google from Sheets (CSV upload secondary) — no connector zoo, no pixels. Optional automation: download the Mcfly pipe template from Spend → Get spend into Mcfly → Optional automation, fill it with SyncWith / Coupler / Supermetrics / Coefficient (you pay those tools — Mcfly does not require them), then import the CSV. Not a “Works with” partnership. Near-term: Meta/Google spend OAuth for retention, still without path/attribution theater. Overview: paste-first spend. Guide: Sheets steps. Sample templates: long example CSV.

Can I automate spend without connecting every ad account to Mcfly?

Yes. Free path is always paste/CSV. If you already pay SyncWith, Coupler, Supermetrics, or Coefficient, point them at Mcfly’s date,channel,amount (or wide Day + channels) template, download the filled CSV, and import on Spend. Mcfly does not sell or embed those connectors. Public examples: long · wide.

My Total ROAS doesn't match what Meta or Google shows. Is something broken?

Almost always no. Ad platforms report conversions their own model credits to a click or view — that number can run well ahead of what actually landed in your bank. Total ROAS only counts money Shopify recorded as sales against money that left for ads, same period, both sides. Send us a screenshot with the period if you want a second look.

What happens to my data if I uninstall?

Shopify sends us a shop-redact webhook and we delete your Mcfly shop record, settings, and spend entries on our side, subject to short backup windows. Full detail in Privacy.

How fast will I hear back?

Usually the same business day. We're a small team reading a real inbox, not routing you through tiers — if something's urgent (numbers look wrong before a Monday budget call), say so in the subject line.

What we refuse to build

No pixels. No MTA. No “true ROAS.” If a request needs path attribution to work, the answer is no — that’s a hard product line, not a missing feature. We measure money spent on ads against money Shopify recorded as sales, full stop.
