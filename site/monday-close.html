What is a Monday Close memo?

A weekly cash ritual for paid media: confirm sales and spend for the same shop-local window, read Total ROAS against break-even, then write one budget decision you can save and export. It is a finance memo — not a path-attribution standup. Full desk: Product.

What number do we manage?

Total ROAS is the Monday number:

Total ROAS = Shopify sales after returns ÷ Total ad spend
Break-even ≈ 1 ÷ contribution margin %

This is average portfolio efficiency — not marginal return, not causal “true ROAS.” Channel path credit is out of scope. Run the math: MER calculator.

How do I run the Monday ritual?

  1. Exceptions — margin confirmed? spend logged? coverage OK?
  2. Save — keep the period numbers so next week can compare.
  3. Variance — vs prior memo (sales, spend, Total ROAS).
  4. Decision — hold / reduce / step-test with a spend floor.
  5. Export — CSV / PDF for the binder / Slack.

How should sales reconcile to Shopify?

Sales after returns should tie to Shopify Reports → Finance for the same shop-local window — what you actually kept, not order totals before returns. Ad spend is the cash that left for media in that window. Platform-attributed “revenue” is not the numerator or denominator for Total ROAS.

What data does Mcfly use?

  • Orders: opaque order ids + order counts as needed — least-privilege; not name/email/phone/address.
  • No Mcfly tracking pixel. No path / view-through credit.
  • Day-one spend: paste from Sheets/CSV. SyncWith-class tools optional (you pay them).
