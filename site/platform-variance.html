Platform claimed vs Shopify banked

Meta and Google report modelled “revenue.” Shopify reports what you banked. Platform variance is that gap — without a pixel or identity graph. Then ask the Monday question: did spend clear break-even on banked sales?

Sample below is anonymized / illustrative (35% margin). Not a customer case study. Live closes use your Total Sales ÷ your logged ad spend.
