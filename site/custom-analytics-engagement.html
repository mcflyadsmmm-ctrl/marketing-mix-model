2. Fixed fee & payment

Total professional fee: $[AMOUNT] USD (fixed) for the scope in §3.

Typical payment schedule (finalized in signed letter): 50% on countersignature · 50% on delivery acceptance (or milestone schedule for Tier III).

Third-party tools, cloud, CRM, or ad-platform licenses billed at cost when required — itemized, never buried in the professional fee.

Package bands on the public site: Close Memo $5–8K · Pipeline Desk $8–15K · Hired System $15–25K. The signed letter states the exact fee for this engagement.

3. Scope (tier selected in signed letter)

One tier is selected. Placeholders below mirror public packages; the signed SOW replaces brackets with named deliverables.

Tier I · $5–8K · ~2–3 weeks

Close Memo

Platform spend rollup, invoice vs UI variance flags, sales/revenue period check, written close memo, 30-day Q&A on the memo.

Tier II · $8–15K · ~3–6 weeks

Pipeline Desk

Metric contracts (lead → MQL → SQL or client stages), spend joined to CRM, CPQL / efficiency vs targets, operator desk + handoff docs, weekly demos during build.

Tier III · $15–25K · ~6–10 weeks

Hired System

Pipelines or Sheet source-of-truth, decision UI, rules-based allocation helpers, runbooks, production handoff. Optional retainer quoted separately at handoff.

4. Included & excluded

Included (typical)

  • Discovery call and written scope confirmation
  • Metric definitions before models or desks ship
  • Build work within the selected tier
  • Weekly demo cadence (Tier II / III)
  • Handoff checklist, credentials time-box, runbooks

Excluded unless addendum

  • Forensic agency / rebate audits (~$100K class)
  • Always-on MMM SaaS parity
  • Warehouse / CDP rebuilds outside named scope
  • Ongoing monitoring without a retainer
  • Work outside the refuse list in §10

5. Client responsibilities

  • Timely read access to ad platforms and invoice / billing exports
  • Sales or CRM stage definitions and a named decision owner
  • Margin or target-efficiency assumptions when allocation is in scope
  • One weekly 30–45 minute touch for Tier II / III during the build window
  • Prompt feedback on demos (delays may shift milestone dates without changing fee unless scope expands)

6. Deliverables & acceptance

Deliverables are those named for the selected tier (memo, desk, pipelines, docs). Work lands in the client’s cloud / Sheets / warehouse when practicable.

Acceptance: client confirms in writing (email sufficient) within [5–10] business days of delivery notice that deliverables materially match the SOW, or lists specific defects. Silence after the acceptance window may be treated as acceptance under the signed letter.

Warranty is limited to correcting material non-conformity to the written SOW within a short post-acceptance window — not open-ended product support unless a retainer is signed.

7. Timeline milestones

Illustrative cadence; dates lock in the signed letter after discovery.

  1. Week 0 Discovery call · access checklist · fee & tier confirmation
  2. Week 0–1 Countersigned engagement letter · kickoff · metric contracts draft
  3. Build Weekly demos (Tier II / III) · variance / desk iteration
  4. Handoff Delivery package · ownership transfer · optional retainer quote

8. Data ownership & privacy

  • Client owns its data and the deliverables created under the SOW (subject to Mcfly’s pre-existing methods, templates, and know-how).
  • Least-privilege, time-boxed credentials; no standing admin unless the SOW says so.
  • No shadow CRM of client customers; no identity-graph product.
  • Public posture: Privacy, Security, draft DPA. Custom NDA / DPA schedules may attach to the signed letter.

9. Change control

Scope changes (new platforms, new CRM objects, additional models, forensic depth, etc.) require a written addendum with fee and timeline impact. Verbal requests do not expand the engagement.

Dirty source data may pause the clock or trigger an addendum for cleanup — called out early, not buried at invoice time.

10. Refuse list

Out of scope always: pixels; multi-touch / path credit; view-through or “true ROAS” identity graphs; Triple Whale–class attribution clones; SyncWith-style connector zoos sold as Mcfly’s product. Operating metric remains outcomes ÷ spend.

Is this a contract?

No. This page is a specimen for buyer education. It is not a contract, offer, or legal advice. Binding terms — fee, scope, liability, IP, governing law — appear only in a countersigned engagement letter. Inquire on the custom hub to start that process.

What's in the letter?

Parties and purpose; fixed fee and payment schedule; one selected scope tier ($5–8K / $8–15K / $15–25K); included vs excluded work; client responsibilities; deliverables and acceptance; timeline milestones; data ownership and privacy; change control; refuse list (no pixels / MTA); signature blocks. Optional Monday-close retainer is quoted separately at handoff — not hourly bait.
