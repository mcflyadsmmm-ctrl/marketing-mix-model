About Mcfly

Founded by Marty Smithson in Utah. One product: a Shopify app that puts ad spend next to sales, day by day.

Mcfly Ads is the Shopify Monday cash desk. Total ROAS = Shopify sales after returns ÷ ad spend for the same period. Break-even from contribution margin. Average portfolio efficiency — not path credit, not “true ROAS.”

The Shopify app

Mcfly Analytics is the whole desk: Spend, Overview, LTV, and Goals. Paste or type spend from any platform, set margin, close the week. Flat $39/store/mo after a 7-day trial via Shopify billing — not a cut of sales.

How we got here

Before Mcfly was a Shopify app, the work was measured ad spend — reconciling what platforms claimed against what the bank saw.

Most Monday budget calls need something faster than consulting theater: one auditable ratio, break-even from margin, and one mix move. That became this app.

What we won’t invent for trust

No customer logo wall. No fabricated testimonials. No pixels, MTA, or path-credit theater. When design partners have quotes worth publishing, they go here with names. Until then: the formula, honest fees, and a human reply.
