About Mcfly Advertising & Analytics

Founded by Marty Smithson in Utah. Two strengths under one company — a Shopify Monday cash desk, and fixed-fee Custom Data Solutions for everyone else.

Two products · one company. Mcfly Advertising & Analytics makes marketing spend defendable against real sales: a Shopify Monday cash desk (Total ROAS = Shopify sales after returns ÷ ad spend), and fixed-fee Custom Data Solutions desks for everyone else.

Shopify Monday cash desk

Pillar A is the Mcfly Analytics Shopify app — a Monday cash desk operators can defend to finance. Total ROAS = Shopify sales after returns ÷ total ad spend for the same period. Break-even from contribution margin. Average portfolio efficiency — not marginal channel return, not causal “true ROAS.”

Paste or type spend from any platform (including billboards), set margin, close the week. Flat Pro fee via Shopify App Pricing for LTV + Goals — not a GMV tax. Try the demo.

Custom Data Solutions

Pillar B is fixed-fee Custom Data Solutions ($5–25K bands) that ship a weekly decision desk and written metric contracts — not always-on MMM theater and not an identity-graph product. For operators who need a desk outside Shopify-only, or a scoped rebuild of spend-and-sales truth.

How we got here

Before Mcfly was a Shopify app, the work was measured ad spend and marketing-mix consulting — reconciling what platforms claimed against what the bank saw.

Custom MMM and regression stacks answer interesting academic questions. Most Monday budget calls need something faster: one auditable ratio, break-even from margin, and one mix move.

So the consulting work became two doors — division on Shopify, and scoped fixed-fee desks when the store isn’t on Shopify. Money against money — same line as our privacy page.

What we won’t invent for trust

No customer logo wall. No fabricated testimonials. No pixels, MTA, or path-credit theater. When design partners have quotes worth publishing, they go here with names. Until then: the formula, honest fees, and a human reply.
