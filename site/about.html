Two products · one firm. Mcfly Ads sells custom data science they keep. Mcfly Analytics is the Shopify app ($39) that puts spend next to sales — and a path into Custom when books still disagree.

Mcfly Analytics (Shopify app)

Pillar A is the Mcfly Analytics Shopify app — spend beside Shopify sales for the same dates. Total ROAS = sales after returns ÷ total ad spend. Break-even from contribution margin. Average portfolio efficiency — not path credit, not “true ROAS.”

Paste or type spend from any platform (including billboards), set margin, close the week. Flat Pro fee via Shopify Billing — not a GMV tax. Try the demo. When invoices, CRM, and cash still disagree, that is Custom.

Custom Data Solutions

Pillar B is fixed-fee Custom Data Solutions ($5–25K) — hired systems they keep: exec desks, role seats, metric contracts, handoff files. For operators who need more than a Shopify desk, or a scoped rebuild of spend-and-sales truth.
