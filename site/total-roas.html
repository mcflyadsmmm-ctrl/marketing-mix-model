Total ROAS = sales ÷ spend you added

Shopify Total Sales after returns, for the dates you picked, divided by every dollar you typed, pasted, or uploaded. That is the desk noun. Searchers still type MER — same ratio, same Monday job.

Definitions

Five terms.
One Monday job.

  • Total ROAS

    Shopify sales after returns divided by spend you added for the same window. Not order totals before returns. Not platform-reported revenue.

    Total ROAS = Shopify Total Sales ÷ spend you added

  • Break-even Total ROAS

    The efficiency floor from your profit margin — below it, ads lose money even if a platform ROAS looks healthy.

    break-even ≈ 1 ÷ profit margin

  • Spend you added

    CSV, type-a-bill, or pasted Ads Manager rows. Offline lines (billboard, retainer, podcast) count the same as Meta or Google.

    exact dollars you entered · same dates as Overview

  • Allocation

    Channel mix and quarterly scoreboard from the same sales ÷ spend math — auditable inputs, one budget conversation.

    if Total ROAS < break-even → protect / shift mix

  • SAMPLE honesty

    Demo numbers on this site (Harbor Home Co) are labeled SAMPLE. They are not a merchant’s live books.

    $82,068 sales ÷ $23,414 spend = 3.51× · SAMPLE
